### 📘 PHYSICS MODULE: SIMPLE HARMONIC MOTION

---

#### ┌── Introduction ────────────────────────────────────┐
│ A **Simple Pendulum** consists of a heavy point mass (bob) suspended from a rigid support by a massless, inextensible string. When pulled to one side and released, it executes periodic motion in a vertical plane under the influence of gravity.
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Key Concepts ─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ • **Restoring Force:** The component of gravity that pulls the bob back to equilibrium.
│ • **Small Angle Approximation:** For oscillations where $\theta < 10^\circ$, we assume $\sin \theta \approx \theta$.
│ • **Effective Length ($L$):** Distance from the point of suspension to the center of gravity of the bob.
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Formula Card: Restoring Force ──────────────────────┐
│ **Title:** Dynamics of the Bob
│ 
│ $$ F_{restoring} = -mg \sin \theta $$
│ 
│ **Variables:**
│ • $m$: Mass of the bob
│ • $g$: Acceleration due to gravity
│ • $\theta$: Angular displacement
│ 
│ [ **Explain:** The negative sign indicates the force acts opposite to displacement. ]
│ [ **Open Formula Lab** ]
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Derivation Steps ──────────────────────────────────┐
│ **1. Applying Newton's Second Law:**
│ The restoring force $F$ is also equal to $ma$. Therefore:
│ $ma = -mg \sin \theta$
│ $a = -g \sin \theta$
│
│ **2. Small Angle Approximation:**
│ For small $\theta$, $\sin \theta \approx \theta$ (in radians).
│ From geometry, $\theta = \frac{x}{L}$ (where $x$ is arc length).
│ $a = -g \left( \frac{x}{L} \right)$
│
│ **3. Relation to SHM:**
│ Standard SHM equation is $a = -\omega^2 x$.
│ Comparing the two: $\omega^2 = \frac{g}{L}$
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Formula Card: Angular Velocity ─────────────────────┐
│ **Title:** System Frequency
│ 
│ $$ \omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L}} $$
│ 
│ **Variables:**
│ • $\omega$: Angular frequency (rad/s)
│ • $g$: $9.8 \, m/s^2$
│ • $L$: Length of string
│ 
│ [ **Explain:** Frequency depends only on length and gravity, not mass. ]
│ [ **Open Formula Lab** ]
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Main Formula: Time Period ─────────────────────────┐
│ **Title:** Period of Oscillation
│ 
│ $$ T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} $$
│ 
│ **Variables:**
│ • $T$: Time for one complete oscillation (s)
│ • $L$: Length of the pendulum (m)
│ • $g$: Local gravitational acceleration
│ 
│ [ **Explain:** This is the "Gallilean" period for small amplitudes. ]
│ [ **Open Formula Lab** ]
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Characteristics ───────────────────────────────────┐
│ | Factor | Effect on Time Period ($T$) |
│ | :--- | :--- |
│ | **Length ($L$)** | $T \propto \sqrt{L}$ (Longer pendulum = Slower) |
│ | **Gravity ($g$)** | $T \propto 1/\sqrt{g}$ (Higher gravity = Faster) |
│ | **Mass ($m$)** | No effect (Independent of mass) |
│ | **Amplitude** | No effect (for small angles) |
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

#### ┌── Important Notes ──────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⚠️ **The "Second's Pendulum":** A pendulum with a time period of exactly 2 seconds. Its length is approximately 0.994 meters on Earth.
│ 
│ ⚠️ **Non-Linearity:** If the angle $\theta$ is large, the formula becomes significantly more complex and the motion is no longer strictly SHM.
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
