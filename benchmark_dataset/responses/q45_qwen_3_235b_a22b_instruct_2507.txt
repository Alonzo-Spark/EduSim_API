│ Quick Definition            │
│                           │
│ **Electrostatic shielding** (also known as **electrostatic screening**) is the phenomenon where a conductive enclosure blocks external static electric fields from reaching its interior. This means that the electric field inside a closed conductor is zero when exposed to external electrostatic fields.  
│  
└───────────────────────────┘
