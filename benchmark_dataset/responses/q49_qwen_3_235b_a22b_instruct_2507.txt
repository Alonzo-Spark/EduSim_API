┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Main Formula              │
│                           │

│                           │
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │

**Given:**  
- Current ($I$) = 2 A  
- Resistance ($R$) = 10 Ω

**To find:**  
- Voltage ($V$) = ?

Using Ohm’s Law:

$$
V = I \times R = 2\, \text{A} \times 10\, \Omega = 20\, \text{V}
$$

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Final Answer              │
│                           │
│ $$ \boxed{20\ \text{V}} $$│
└───────────────────────────┘
