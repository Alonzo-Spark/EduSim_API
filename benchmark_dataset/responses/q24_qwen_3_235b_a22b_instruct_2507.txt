┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Quick Definition          │  
│                           │  
│ Gravitational force is the attractive force between two masses due to gravity, described by Newton’s Law of Universal Gravitation.  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Main Formula              │  
│                           │  
$$
F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}
$$  
│                           │  
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │  
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### Key Concepts  
- **Gravitational Constant**: $ G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \ \text{N·m}^2/\text{kg}^2 $  
- $ m_1, m_2 $: masses of the two objects (in kg)  
- $ r $: distance between their centers (in m)  
- The force is always attractive and acts along the line joining the centers.

---

### Given Values  
| Variable | Value               |  
|---------|---------------------|  
| $ m_1 $ | 5 kg                |  
| $ m_2 $ | 5 kg                |  
| $ r $   | 2 m                 |  
| $ G $   | $ 6.674 \times 10^{-11} $ |  

---

### Step-by-Step Calculation  

1. Substitute values into the formula:  
$$
F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{(5)(5)}{(2)^2}
$$

2. Simplify numerator and denominator:  
$$
F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \frac{25}{4} = (6.674 \times 10^{-11}) \times 6.25
$$

3. Multiply:  
$$
F = 4.17125 \times 10^{-10} \ \text{N}
$$

---

### Final Answer  
┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Result                    │  
│                           │  
$$
F \approx 4.17 \times 10^{-10} \ \text{N}
$$  
│                           │  
│ This is a very small force — typical for everyday-sized masses.  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### Important Notes  
- Gravitational force between ordinary objects is extremely weak.  
- This force becomes significant only when at least one mass is very large (e.g., planets or stars).  
- The result is rounded to three significant figures, matching the precision of the given data.
