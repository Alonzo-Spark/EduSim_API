<div style="font-size: 16px; color: #cbd5e1; line-height: 1.6;">When a metal rod is heated, its atoms vibrate faster and push apart — causing the rod to grow longer. This is thermal expansion.</div>

### 📘 Quick Definition

<div style="background: #f0f9ff; border-left: 4px solid #3b82f6; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin-bottom: 20px;">

**Thermal Expansion** is the tendency of matter to **change its dimensions** (length, area, or volume) in response to a **change in temperature**.

> When heated → particles gain kinetic energy → vibrate more → occupy more space → material expands.

</div>

---

### ⚛️ Why Does It Happen?

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 16px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

<div style="background: #fefce8; border: 1px solid #fde047; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px;">
<div style="font-weight: 700; color: #854d0e; margin-bottom: 8px;">🧊 Before Heating</div>
<div style="font-size: 14px; color: #713f12;">Atoms vibrate slowly around fixed positions. Interatomic distances are at equilibrium.</div>
</div>

<div style="background: #fff7ed; border: 1px solid #fb923c; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px;">
<div style="font-weight: 700; color: #9a3412; margin-bottom: 8px;">🔥 After Heating</div>
<div style="font-size: 14px; color: #7c2d12;">Atoms gain kinetic energy, vibrate faster and with greater amplitude, pushing neighbors further apart.</div>
</div>

</div>

---

### 📐 Three Types of Thermal Expansion

<div style="background: white; border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 12px; overflow: hidden; margin-bottom: 24px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06);">

<div style="background: #1e293b; color: white; padding: 14px 20px; font-weight: 700; font-size: 15px;">Types of Thermal Expansion</div>

| Type | Dimension | Applies To | Symbol |
|------|-----------|------------|--------|
| **Linear** | Length (1D) | Rods, wires, rails | α |
| **Superficial** | Area (2D) | Plates, sheets | β |
| **Volumetric** | Volume (3D) | Liquids, gases, solids | γ |

</div>

### 🔢 Formula Cards

<div style="background: white; border: 2px solid #6366f1; border-radius: 14px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 16px; box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(99,102,241,0.15);">

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: center; margin-bottom: 16px;">
<div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 17px; color: #4338ca;">📌 Linear Expansion</div>
<div style="background: #eef2ff; color: #4338ca; padding: 4px 12px; border-radius: 20px; font-size: 12px; font-weight: 600;">1D</div>
</div>

$$\Delta L = L_0 \cdot \alpha \cdot \Delta T$$

<div style="background: #f8fafc; border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; margin-top: 16px;">
<div style="font-weight: 600; color: #475569; margin-bottom: 10px;">Variable Guide</div>

| Symbol | Meaning | Unit |
|--------|---------|------|
| $\Delta L$ | Change in length | m |
| $L_0$ | Original length | m |
| $\alpha$ | Coefficient of linear expansion | °C⁻¹ |
| $\Delta T$ | Change in temperature | °C or K |

</div>

</div>

<div style="background: white; border: 2px solid #10b981; border-radius: 14px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 16px; box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(16,185,129,0.15);">

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: center; margin-bottom: 16px;">
<div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 17px; color: #065f46;">📌 Volumetric Expansion</div>
<div style="background: #ecfdf5; color: #065f46; padding: 4px 12px; border-radius: 20px; font-size: 12px; font-weight: 600;">3D</div>
</div>

$$\Delta V = V_0 \cdot \gamma \cdot \Delta T$$

<div style="background: #f8fafc; border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; margin-top: 16px;">
<div style="font-weight: 600; color: #475569; margin-bottom: 10px;">Variable Guide</div>

| Symbol | Meaning | Unit |
|--------|---------|------|
| $\Delta V$ | Change in volume | m³ |
| $V_0$ | Original volume | m³ |
| $\gamma$ | Coefficient of volumetric expansion | °C⁻¹ |
| $\Delta T$ | Change in temperature | °C or K |

</div>

</div>

### 🔗 Relationship Between Coefficients

<div style="background: #fdf4ff; border: 2px solid #d946ef; border-radius: 14px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

<div style="font-weight: 700; color: #7e22ce; margin-bottom: 16px; font-size: 16px;">📌 Key Relationship</div>

$$\alpha : \beta : \gamma = 1 : 2 : 3$$

<div style="margin-top: 16px; font-size: 14px; color: #6b21a8;">
This means: &nbsp; $\beta = 2\alpha$ &nbsp; and &nbsp; $\gamma = 3\alpha$
</div>

</div>

### 💡 Important Notes

<div style="background: #fffbeb; border: 1px solid #fcd34d; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

<div style="font-weight: 700; color: #92400e; margin-bottom: 12px;">⚠️ Remember These</div>

<ul style="color: #78350f; line-height: 2; font-size: 14px; padding-left: 20px;">
<li>Gases expand <strong>much more</strong> than liquids, which expand more than solids.</li>
<li>Water is an <strong>anomalous exception</strong> — it contracts from 0°C to 4°C, then expands.</li>
<li>The coefficient α is a <strong>material property</strong> — different for steel, copper, glass, etc.</li>
<li>Expansion is <strong>reversible</strong> — the material contracts back on cooling.</li>
</ul>
