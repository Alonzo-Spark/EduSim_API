To find the range of a projectile launched at an angle, we can use the formula:

\[
R = \frac{v^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}
\]

where:
- \( R \) is the range,
- \( v \) is the initial velocity,
- \( \theta \) is the launch angle,
- \( g \) is the acceleration due to gravity (approximately \( 9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2 \)).

### Step-by-step Calculation

1. **Given Data**:
   - Initial velocity, \( v = 20 \, \text{m/s} \)
   - Launch angle, \( \theta = 45^\circ \)
   - Acceleration due to gravity, \( g = 9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2 \)

2. **Calculate the Range**:

   Using the formula:
   \[
   R = \frac{(20)^2 \sin(2 \times 45^\circ)}{9.81}
   \]

   We know that \( \sin(90^\circ) = 1 \), thus:
   \[
   R = \frac{400 \times 1}{9.81} \approx \frac{400}{9.81} \approx 40.8 \, \text{m}
   \]

### Result
The range of the projectile is approximately **40.8 meters**.
