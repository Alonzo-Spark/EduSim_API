┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Quick Definition**      │  
│                           │  
│ Potential energy (PE) is the energy an object possesses due to its position in a gravitational field. When a body is lifted against gravity, it gains gravitational potential energy. │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Key Concept**           │  
│                           │  
│ Gravitational Potential Energy depends on mass, gravitational acceleration, and height above a reference point. │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Main Formula**          │  
│                           │  
│     $$  
PE = mgh  
$$      │  
│                           │  
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │  
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### **Given Values**  
- Mass ($m$) = 5 kg  
- Height ($h$) = 10 m  
- Gravitational acceleration ($g$) = 9.8 m/s²  

---

### **Calculation**  
Using the formula:  
$$  
PE = mgh = 5 \times 9.8 \times 10  
$$  
$$  
PE = 490 \text{ joules}  
$$  

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Final Answer**          │  
│                           │  
│ The potential energy of the body is **490 J**. │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### **Important Notes**  
- If approximate value $g = 10 \, \text{m/s}^2$ is used:  
  $PE = 5 \times 10 \times 10 = 500 \, \text{J}$  
- Always specify the value of $g$ used in calculations.  
- Potential energy is measured in **joules (J)**.
