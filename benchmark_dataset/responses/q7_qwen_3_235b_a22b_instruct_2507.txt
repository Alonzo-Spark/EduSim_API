┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Quick Definition          │
│                           │
│ The **maximum height** of a projectile is the highest vertical position reached during its trajectory, occurring when the vertical component of velocity becomes zero. │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Key Concepts              │
│                           │
│ • Projectile motion follows a parabolic path under gravity.  
│ • Motion can be split into horizontal and vertical components.  
│ • At maximum height, vertical velocity $ v_y = 0 $.  
│ • Acceleration in the vertical direction is $ -g $ (downward). │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Formula                   │
│                           │
│     $$                    │
│     H_{\text{max}} = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g}  
\\  
│     $$                    │
│                           │
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

### Step-by-Step Derivation

We consider a projectile launched with:

- Initial speed: $ u $
- Launch angle: $ \theta $
- Acceleration due to gravity: $ g $ (acting downward)

#### 1. **Resolve Initial Velocity**

Break the initial velocity into vertical and horizontal components:

- Horizontal component: $ u_x = u \cos\theta $
- Vertical component: $ u_y = u \sin\theta $

Only the vertical component affects the height.

#### 2. **Use Equation of Motion**

We use the kinematic equation (no time involved):

$$
v_y^2 = u_y^2 - 2gH
$$

At maximum height $ H_{\text{max}} $, the vertical velocity becomes zero:  
$ v_y = 0 $

Substitute:

$$
0 = (u \sin\theta)^2 - 2gH_{\text{max}}
$$

#### 3. **Solve for $ H_{\text{max}} $**

$$
2gH_{\text{max}} = u^2 \sin^2\theta
$$

$$
H_{\text{max}} = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g}
$$

---

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Important Notes           │
│                           │
│ • Maximum height depends only on the vertical component of velocity.  
│ • For a given speed, height is maximized when $ \theta = 90^\circ $ (vertical throw).  
│ • At $ \theta = 90^\circ $, $ \sin\theta = 1 $, so $ H_{\text{max}} = \frac{u^2}{2g} $.  
│ • Horizontal motion does not affect maximum height. │
└───────────────────────────┘
