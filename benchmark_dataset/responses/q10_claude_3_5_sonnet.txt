# Scalar vs Vector Quantities

<div class="section-grid">

<div class="concept-card full-width">
<div class="card-header intro-header">
<span class="card-icon">📖</span>
<span class="card-title">Overview</span>
</div>
<div class="card-body">
Physical quantities in physics are classified into two fundamental categories based on whether they require a <strong>direction</strong> to be fully described. Understanding this distinction is foundational to all of classical mechanics.
</div>
</div>

</div>

---

<div class="section-grid two-col">

<div class="concept-card">
<div class="card-header scalar-header">
<span class="card-icon">🔢</span>
<span class="card-title">Scalar Quantity</span>
</div>
<div class="card-body">
<p>A quantity that is <strong>completely described by magnitude alone</strong> — no direction is needed.</p>

<div class="property-list">
<div class="property-item">
<span class="property-label">Representation</span>
<span class="property-value">Simple number with unit</span>
</div>
<div class="property-item">
<span class="property-label">Direction</span>
<span class="property-value">❌ Not required</span>
</div>
<div class="property-item">
<span class="property-label">Algebra</span>
<span class="property-value">Ordinary arithmetic</span>
</div>
</div>

<div class="example-box scalar-example">
<div class="example-title">Examples</div>
<div class="example-tags">
<span class="tag">Mass — 5 kg</span>
<span class="tag">Temperature — 37°C</span>
<span class="tag">Speed — 60 km/h</span>
<span class="tag">Energy — 200 J</span>
<span class="tag">Time — 10 s</span>
<span class="tag">Distance — 100 m</span>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="concept-card">
<div class="card-header vector-header">
<span class="card-icon">➡️</span>
<span class="card-title">Vector Quantity</span>
</div>
<div class="card-body">
<p>A quantity that requires both <strong>magnitude AND direction</strong> for complete description.</p>

<div class="property-list">
<div class="property-item">
<span class="property-label">Representation</span>
<span class="property-value">Arrow or bold symbol <strong>F</strong></span>
</div>
<div class="property-item">
<span class="property-label">Direction</span>
<span class="property-value">✅ Essential</span>
</div>
<div class="property-item">
<span class="property-label">Algebra</span>
<span class="property-value">Vector addition rules</span>
</div>
</div>

<div class="example-box vector-example">
<div class="example-title">Examples</div>
<div class="example-tags">
<span class="tag">Force — 10 N East</span>
<span class="tag">Velocity — 60 km/h North</span>
<span class="tag">Displacement — 5 m upward</span>
<span class="tag">Acceleration — 9.8 m/s² down</span>
<span class="tag">Momentum — 20 kg·m/s</span>
<span class="tag">Electric Field</span>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

---

<div class="formula-card">
<div class="formula-card-header">
<span class="formula-icon">📐</span>
<span class="formula-title">Vector Magnitude Formula</span>
</div>
<div class="formula-body">
<div class="formula-display">

$$|\vec{A}| = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}$$

</div>
<div class="formula-legend">
<div class="legend-row">
<span class="legend-symbol">$$|\vec{A}|$$</span>
<span class="legend-desc">Magnitude (scalar value) of vector <strong>A</strong></span>
</div>
<div class="legend-row">
<span class="legend-symbol">$$A_x, A_y, A_z$$</span>
<span class="legend-desc">Components along x, y, z axes</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="formula-note">
💡 Notice: the result of this formula is a <em>scalar</em> — magnitude has no direction.
</div>
</div>
</div>

---

<div class="concept-card full-width">
<div class="card-header key-header">
<span class="card-icon">⚡</span>
<span class="card-title">Key Conceptual Difference — Speed vs Velocity</span>
</div>
<div class="card-body">
<div class="comparison-scenario">
<div class="scenario-box">
<div class="scenario-label scalar-label">SCALAR</div>
<div class="scenario-content">
<strong>Speed = 60 km/h</strong><br/>
Tells you <em>how fast</em> — nothing more.
</div>
</div>
<div class="scenario-arrow">≠</div>
<div class="scenario-box">
<div class="scenario-label vector-label">VECTOR</div>
<div class="scenario-content">
<strong>Velocity = 60 km/h North</strong><br/>
Tells you <em>how fast</em> AND <em>where to</em>.
</div>
</div>
</div>
<p class="scenario-note">A car going East at 60 km/h and a car going West at 60 km/h have the <strong>same speed</strong> but <strong>opposite velocities</strong>.</p>
</div>
</div>

---

<div class="table-card">
<div class="card-header table-header">
<span class="card-icon">📊</span>
<span class="card-title">Comparison Table</span>
</div>
<div class="table-wrapper">
<table class="styled-table">
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Property</th>
<th>Scalar</th>
<th>Vector</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Definition</td>
<td>Magnitude only</td>
<td>Magnitude + Direction</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Symbol</td>
<td>A</td>
<td><strong>A</strong> or Ā</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Addition Rule</td>
<td>Simple arithmetic</td>
<td>Triangle / Parallelogram law</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Division</td>
<td>Always possible</td>
<td>Not defined between vectors</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Effect of direction</td>
<td>No effect</td>
<td>Completely changes the quantity</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Example pair</td>
<td>Distance</td>
<td>Displacement</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>
</div>

<div class="section-grid two-col">

<div class="concept-card">
<div class="card-header note-header">
<span class="card-icon">📝</span>
<span class="card-title">Important Note</span>
</div>
<div class="card-body">
<p>Some quantities appear similar but belong to different categories:</p>
<ul class="styled-list">
<li><strong>Distance</strong> (scalar) vs <strong>Displacement</strong> (vector)</li>
<li><strong>Speed</strong> (scalar) vs <strong>Velocity</strong> (vector)</li>
<li><strong>Mass</strong> (scalar) vs <strong>Weight</strong> (vector)</li>
<li><strong>Energy</strong> (scalar) vs <strong>Force</strong> (vector)</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<div class="concept-card">
<div class="card-header summary-header">
<span class="card-icon">✅</span>
<span class="card-title">Quick Memory Rule</span>
</div>
<div class="card-body">
<div class="memory-rule">
<div class="rule-box">
<span class="rule-icon">🔢</span>
<div>
<strong>Scalar</strong><br/>
Ask: <em>"How much?"</em><br/>
One number answers it fully.
</div>
</div>
<div class="rule-box">
<span class="rule-icon">➡️</span>
<div>
<strong>Vector</strong><br/>
Ask: <em>"How much AND which way?"</em><br/>
Direction is non-negotiable.
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
