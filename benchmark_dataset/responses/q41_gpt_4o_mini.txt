### Important Notes
- **Variables**:
  - \( F \): Magnitude of the electrostatic force between two charges.
  - \( k \): Coulomb's constant (\( 8.99 \times 10^9 \, \text{N m}^2/\text{C}^2 \)).
  - \( q_1 \) and \( q_2 \): Magnitudes of the charges.
  - \( r \): Distance between the centers of the two charges.

### Summary
Coulomb's law quantifies the electrostatic force between two charged particles, showing that the force is directly proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
