│ uniformly from rest, its  │  
│ velocity increases at a   │  
│ constant rate over time.  │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Main Formula**          │  
│                           │  
│ $$                        │  
│ v = u + at                │

---

### **Given Values**

- Initial velocity ($u$) = 0 m/s *(since the car starts from rest)*  
- Acceleration ($a$) = 2 m/s²  
- Time ($t$) = 10 s  

---

### **Apply the Formula**

$$
v = u + at = 0 + (2)(10) = 20 \text{ m/s}
$$

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Final Answer**          │  
│                           │  
│ The car's velocity after  │  
│ 10 seconds is **20 m/s**. │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### **Key Concept**
Uniform acceleration means the velocity changes by the same amount each second. Here, the car gains 2 m/s every second, so after 10 seconds:  
$2 \times 10 = 20$ m/s.
