**Key Concepts**

*   **Potential Energy (PE):** The energy stored in an object due to its position relative to a force field (like gravity).
*   **Mass (m):** The amount of matter in the object (measured in kilograms, kg).
*   **Gravity (g):** The acceleration due to gravity (approximately 9.8 m/s² on Earth).
*   **Height (h):** The vertical distance the object is lifted (measured in meters, m).

**Problem Breakdown**

We are given:

*   Mass (m) = 5 kg
*   Height (h) = 10 m
*   Acceleration due to gravity (g) = 9.8 m/s²

**Calculation**

Using the formula $$PE = mgh$$, we can substitute the given values:

$$PE = (5 \text{ kg}) \cdot (9.8 \text{ m/s}^2) \cdot (10 \text{ m})$$

$$PE = 490 \text{ Joules}$$

**Answer**

The potential energy of the body is 490 Joules.

**Summary**

We successfully calculated the potential energy of an object by applying the formula $$PE = mgh$$. This demonstrates how potential energy increases with mass, height, and the strength of the gravitational field.
