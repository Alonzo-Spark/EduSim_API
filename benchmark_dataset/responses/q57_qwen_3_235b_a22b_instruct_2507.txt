### 🔄 System Setup

A **forced harmonic oscillator** consists of:
- A mass on a spring (natural frequency $ \omega_0 $)
- A damping force (friction/resistance)
- An external driving force with adjustable frequency $ \omega $

The equation of motion is:

$$
m\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + b\frac{dx}{dt} + kx = F_0 \cos(\omega t)
$$

### 👁️ Visual Effect of Increasing Driven Frequency

Drag the slider below in your mind (or imagine a live simulation):

#### 🟦 Low Driven Frequency ($ \omega \ll \omega_0 $)
- The driving force changes **very slowly**
- The mass moves **in phase** with the driver
- **Amplitude is moderate**, follows static response
- Motion looks smooth and synchronized

> 🎯 *Visual:* Mass gently rocks back and forth, keeping perfect time with the slow push.

---

#### 🟨 Near Resonance ($ \omega \approx \omega_0 $)
- Driven frequency approaches natural frequency
- **Amplitude peaks dramatically**
- System absorbs maximum energy
- **Phase lag** of about $ 90^\circ $ develops

> ⚠️ *Visual:* Oscillations grow larger and larger—possibly violent if damping is low. Like pushing a swing at just the right time.

---

#### 🟥 High Driven Frequency ($ \omega \gg \omega_0 $)
- Driver oscillates much faster than system can respond
- **Amplitude drops sharply**
- Mass barely moves—“can’t keep up”
- **Phase opposition**: mass moves *opposite* to the driver

> 🎯 *Visual:* Rapid shaking, but the mass just quivers slightly, lagging behind by nearly half a cycle.

Where:
- $ A = \frac{F_0/m}{\sqrt{(\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)^2 + (b\omega/m)^2}} $ → amplitude depends on $ \omega $
- $ \phi $ = phase difference between driver and response

---

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Resonance Behavior Table  │
└───────────────────────────┘

| Driven Freq $ \omega $ | Amplitude | Phase Lag | Visual Response         |
|------------------------|-----------|-----------|--------------------------|
| $ \ll \omega_0 $       | Moderate  | ~0°       | Smooth, in-step motion   |
| $ \approx \omega_0 $   | Maximum   | ~90°      | Large, energetic swings  |
| $ \gg \omega_0 $       | Small     | ~180°     | Weak, out-of-sync jitter |

---

💡 **Note:** Damping controls how sharp the resonance peak is. Lower damping → taller, narrower peak.
