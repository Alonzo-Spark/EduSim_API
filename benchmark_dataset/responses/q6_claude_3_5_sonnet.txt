Here's the interactive lesson on **Projectile Motion**:

---

## 📖 Quick Definition

> **Projectile Motion** is a form of motion experienced by an object that is launched into the air and moves under the influence of gravity alone (after the initial launch), following a curved path called a **trajectory**.

---

## ⚡ Key Characteristics

| # | Characteristic | Details |
|---|---------------|---------|
| 1 | **Path Shape** | Always a **parabola** |
| 2 | **Acceleration** | Only gravity acts → $a_x = 0$, $a_y = -g$ |
| 3 | **Horizontal Motion** | Uniform (constant velocity) |
| 4 | **Vertical Motion** | Uniformly accelerated (like free fall) |
| 5 | **Independence** | Horizontal & vertical motions are **independent** |
| 6 | **Launch Angle** | Determines range, height, and time of flight |

---

## 🔢 Core Equations

### Horizontal Motion

$$x = u\cos\theta \cdot t$$

### Vertical Motion

$$y = u\sin\theta \cdot t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2$$

### Velocity Components at time *t*

$$v_x = u\cos\theta \qquad v_y = u\sin\theta - gt$$

---

## 📐 Derived Formulas

### 🔹 Time of Flight

$$T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$$

> Total time the projectile stays in the air.

---

### 🔹 Maximum Height

$$H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$$

> Reached when vertical velocity becomes **zero**.

---

### 🔹 Horizontal Range

$$R = \frac{u^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$$

> Maximum when $\theta = 45°$

---

## 🏆 Special Angle Results

| Launch Angle $\theta$ | Range $R$ | Height $H$ | Notes |
|----------------------|-----------|------------|-------|
| $15°$ | $\frac{u^2}{2g}$ | Low | Short range |
| $30°$ | $\frac{u^2\sqrt{3}}{2g}$ | Medium | — |
| $45°$ | $\frac{u^2}{g}$ | Medium | **Maximum Range** ✓ |
| $60°$ | $\frac{u^2\sqrt{3}}{2g}$ | High | Same range as 30° |
| $90°$ | $0$ | Maximum | Straight up, falls back |

> **Note:** Angles that are **complementary** (add to 90°) give the **same horizontal range**.

---

## 🔗 Equation of Trajectory

Eliminating $t$ from the $x$ and $y$ equations:

$$y = x\tan\theta - \frac{gx^2}{2u^2\cos^2\theta}$$

This is of the form $y = ax - bx^2$ → a **downward parabola**, confirming the parabolic path.

---

## 📦 Variable Reference

| Symbol | Meaning | SI Unit |
|--------|---------|---------|
| $u$ | Initial speed | m/s |
| $\theta$ | Launch angle | degrees / radians |
| $g$ | Acceleration due to gravity ($9.8$) | m/s² |
| $t$ | Time elapsed | s |
| $T$ | Time of flight | s |
| $H$ | Maximum height | m |
| $R$ | Horizontal range | m |

---

## 🌍 Real-World Applications

- 🏏 Cricket ball / baseball trajectory
- 💣 Artillery and ballistics
- 🚀 Rocket launches (initial phase)
- ⚽ Kicking a football
- 🎯 Long jump in athletics

---

## ⚠️ Important Assumptions

> 1. Air resistance is **neglected**
> 2. $g$ is **constant** throughout the motion
> 3. The Earth's surface is **flat** (valid for short ranges)
> 4. The projectile is treated as a **point mass**

---

## 💡 Quick Memory Tips

- **Horizontal** → Think *train on flat track* → constant speed
- **Vertical** → Think *free fall* → gravity pulls it down
- **45°** → Always gives **maximum range**
- **Complementary angles** → **Same range**
