┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Main Formula              │  
│                           │  
│     Rendered Formula      │  
│                           │

└───────────────────────────┘  

### Given:
- Force (\( F \)) = 50 N  
- Mass (\( m \)) = 10 kg  

### To find:
- Acceleration (\( a \))

### Calculation:
Using the formula:

\[
a = \frac{F}{m}
\]

Substituting the given values:

\[
a = \frac{50 \, \text{N}}{10 \, \text{kg}} = 5 \, \text{m/s}^2
\]

│ Result                   │  
│                           │
