<div class="tutor-response">

<div class="section-card definition-card">
  <div class="card-label">📖 Pascal's Law</div>
  <div class="card-content">
    <p>Pressure applied to an <strong>enclosed fluid at rest</strong> is transmitted <strong>equally and undiminished</strong> in all directions throughout the fluid and acts perpendicular to the surfaces of the container.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-card formula-card">
  <div class="card-label">⚗️ Mathematical Form</div>
  <div class="formula-display">
    $$\Delta P = \rho \, g \, \Delta h$$
  </div>
  <div class="formula-variables">
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var-sym">ΔP</span><span class="var-desc">Change in pressure (Pa)</span></div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var-sym">ρ</span><span class="var-desc">Density of the fluid (kg/m³)</span></div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var-sym">g</span><span class="var-desc">Acceleration due to gravity (m/s²)</span></div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var-sym">Δh</span><span class="var-desc">Change in height/depth (m)</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="formula-note">
    For a <em>static enclosed fluid</em>, if external pressure is applied, it adds equally at every point — the ρgΔh term accounts only for the hydrostatic contribution.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-card formula-card">
  <div class="card-label">⚙️ Hydraulic Principle (Derived from Pascal's Law)</div>
  <div class="formula-display">
    $$\frac{F_1}{A_1} = \frac{F_2}{A_2} \implies F_2 = F_1 \cdot \frac{A_2}{A_1}$$
  </div>
  <div class="formula-variables">
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var-sym">F₁, F₂</span><span class="var-desc">Forces on piston 1 and piston 2 (N)</span></div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var-sym">A₁, A₂</span><span class="var-desc">Cross-sectional areas of pistons (m²)</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="formula-note">
    A small force on a small piston produces a <strong>large force</strong> on a larger piston — the basis of all hydraulic machines.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-card concept-card">
  <div class="card-label">💡 Key Conditions</div>
  <div class="conditions-grid">
    <div class="condition-item">
      <span class="condition-icon">✅</span>
      <span>Fluid must be <strong>enclosed</strong></span>
    </div>
    <div class="condition-item">
      <span class="condition-icon">✅</span>
      <span>Fluid must be <strong>at rest</strong> (static)</span>
    </div>
    <div class="condition-item">
      <span class="condition-icon">✅</span>
      <span>Fluid must be <strong>incompressible</strong></span>
    </div>
    <div class="condition-item">
      <span class="condition-icon">✅</span>
      <span>Pressure transmitted <strong>equally in all directions</strong></span>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-card applications-card">
  <div class="card-label">🔧 Applications</div>
  <div class="app-table-wrapper">
    <table class="app-table">
      <thead>
        <tr>
          <th>#</th>
          <th>Application</th>
          <th>How Pascal's Law is Used</th>
        </tr>
      </thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td>1</td>
          <td><strong>Hydraulic Lift / Jack</strong></td>
          <td>Small input force on a small piston lifts heavy loads via a larger piston</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>2</td>
          <td><strong>Hydraulic Brakes</strong></td>
          <td>Brake pedal pressure is transmitted equally to all four wheel cylinders</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>3</td>
          <td><strong>Hydraulic Press</strong></td>
          <td>Used in industries to compress, shape, or punch materials with large force</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>4</td>
          <td><strong>Artesian Wells</strong></td>
          <td>Underground water rises due to pressure transmitted through confined aquifers</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>5</td>
          <td><strong>Aircraft Control Systems</strong></td>
          <td>Hydraulic actuators move control surfaces (flaps, rudders) using fluid pressure</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td>6</td>
          <td><strong>Dentist's Chair / Hospital Beds</strong></td>
          <td>Smooth height adjustment using hydraulic cylinders</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-card summary-card">
  <div class="card-label">📌 One-Line Summary</div>
  <div class="summary-text">
    Pascal's Law states that pressure in a confined fluid spreads equally everywhere — this simple idea powers hydraulic machines that multiply force by hundreds of times.
  </div>
</div>
