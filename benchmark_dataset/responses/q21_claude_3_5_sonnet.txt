## Gravitation

---

### Quick Definition

> **Gravitation** is the universal phenomenon by which all objects with mass attract one another. It governs everything from falling apples to orbiting planets.

---

### Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

**Statement:**

Every particle in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that is:
- **Directly proportional** to the product of their masses
- **Inversely proportional** to the **square of the distance** between them
- Directed along the line joining the two particles

This force acts **along the line joining the two bodies** and is always **attractive** in nature.

---

### Main Formula

$$F = G \dfrac{m_1 \, m_2}{r^2}$$

| Symbol | Meaning | SI Unit |
|--------|---------|---------|
| $F$ | Gravitational Force between two bodies | Newton (N) |
| $G$ | Universal Gravitational Constant | $\text{N m}^2 \text{kg}^{-2}$ |
| $m_1$ | Mass of first body | kilogram (kg) |
| $m_2$ | Mass of second body | kilogram (kg) |
| $r$ | Distance between the two bodies | metre (m) |

---

### Value of G

$$G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \ \text{N m}^2 \text{ kg}^{-2}$$

> $G$ is a **universal constant** — its value is the same everywhere in the universe, regardless of the medium, temperature, or the nature of the masses.

---

### Proportionality Form

The law can be expressed in two proportionality steps:

$$F \propto m_1 m_2 \quad \text{(fixed } r\text{)}$$

$$F \propto \dfrac{1}{r^2} \quad \text{(fixed masses)}$$

Combining both:

$$F \propto \dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$

Introducing the constant of proportionality $G$:

$$\boxed{F = G \dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}}$$

---

### Key Characteristics of Gravitational Force

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Nature** | Always attractive, never repulsive |
| **Medium** | Independent of the medium between masses |
| **Range** | Infinite range (long-range force) |
| **Type** | Central force (acts along line joining bodies) |
| **Mutual** | Equal and opposite on both bodies (Newton's 3rd Law) |
| **Weakest force** | Weakest of the four fundamental forces |

---

### Inverse Square Law — Visualised

| Distance $r$ | Force $F$ |
|-------------|-----------|
| $r$ | $F$ |
| $2r$ | $F/4$ |
| $3r$ | $F/9$ |
| $4r$ | $F/16$ |

> If distance **doubles**, force becomes **one-fourth**. This is the **inverse square** relationship.

---

### Important Notes

> ⚠️ **Newton's Law of Gravitation applies strictly between point masses.** For extended bodies, it applies when the distance between them is very large compared to their sizes, or when they are uniform spheres (treated as point masses at their centres).

> ⚠️ The law is **not valid** at extremely small (quantum) scales — where General Relativity or Quantum Gravity takes over.

---

### Quick Summary

- Every mass attracts every other mass.
- Force $\propto$ product of masses, $\propto$ inverse square of distance.
- $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \ \text{N m}^2 \text{kg}^{-2}$
- Force is central, mutual, and always attractive.
- Independent of the medium.
