┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Quick Definition                                                                          │
│                                                                                           │
│ Restitution is a dimensionless coefficient that quantifies the elasticity of a collision. │
│ It is defined as the ratio of the relative speed of two bodies after impact to the speed │
│ before impact:                                                                             │
│                                                                                           │
│ $$e = \frac{v_{\text{after}}}{v_{\text{before}}}$$                                        │
│                                                                                           │
│ A value of 1 indicates a perfectly elastic collision, while 0 represents a perfectly   │
│ inelastic collision.                                                                       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Key Concepts                                                                              │
│                                                                                           │
│ • **Relative velocity** – The velocity difference between the two bodies at the instant │
│   of contact.                                                                             │
│ • **Energy loss** – The portion of kinetic energy that is not recovered after the      │
│   collision.                                                                             │
│ • **Coefficient of restitution (e)** – Ranges from 0 (no bounce) to 1 (full bounce).   │
│ • **Effect on bounce** – Higher e → higher rebound velocity → more bounce.              │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Summary                                                                                   │
│                                                                                           │
│ • Restitution governs how much kinetic energy is conserved during a collision.           │
│ • It is a ratio of post‑collision to pre‑collision relative speeds.                       │
│ • Raising e increases rebound velocity and bounce height, while lowering e reduces both │
│   and can lead to a sticking or rolling outcome.                                          │
