┌─────────────────────────────┐
│           Definition         │
│                             │
│ • **Wavelength**: The distance between successive crests (or troughs) of a wave. It is the spatial period of the wave. │
│ • **Frequency**: The number of complete oscillations (cycles) that pass a fixed point per unit time, measured in hertz (Hz). │
│ • **Amplitude**: The maximum displacement of a point on the wave from its equilibrium position; it determines the wave’s intensity. │
└─────────────────────────────┘
