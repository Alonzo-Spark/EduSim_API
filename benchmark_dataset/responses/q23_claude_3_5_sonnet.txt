<!-- QUICK DEFINITION CARDS -->
<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 16px; margin-bottom: 24px;">

  <div style="background: #ffffff; border-radius: 12px; padding: 20px; border-left: 4px solid #4361ee; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.07);">
    <div style="font-size: 11px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 1px; color: #4361ee; margin-bottom: 8px;">⚛️ Mass</div>
    <div style="font-size: 15px; font-weight: 600; color: #1a1a2e; margin-bottom: 6px;">The amount of matter in an object.</div>
    <div style="font-size: 14px; color: #6c757d; line-height: 1.6;">It is an <strong>intrinsic property</strong> — it does not change regardless of location, gravity, or environment.</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #ffffff; border-radius: 12px; padding: 20px; border-left: 4px solid #f72585; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.07);">
    <div style="font-size: 11px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 1px; color: #f72585; margin-bottom: 8px;">🌍 Weight</div>
    <div style="font-size: 15px; font-weight: 600; color: #1a1a2e; margin-bottom: 6px;">The gravitational force acting on that mass.</div>
    <div style="font-size: 14px; color: #6c757d; line-height: 1.6;">It is a <strong>force</strong> — it changes depending on the strength of gravity at your location.</div>
  </div>

</div>

<!-- REAL WORLD EXAMPLE -->
<div style="background: #ffffff; border-radius: 12px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 24px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.07);">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 1px; color: #06d6a0; margin-bottom: 16px;">🚀 Real-World Example — Astronaut on the Moon</div>

  <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 16px;">

    <div style="background: #f0fff8; border-radius: 10px; padding: 16px; border: 1px solid #b7f5d8;">
      <div style="font-weight: 700; color: #1a1a2e; margin-bottom: 8px;">⚛️ Mass stays constant</div>
      <div style="font-size: 14px; color: #495057; line-height: 1.6;">An astronaut with a mass of <strong>70 kg</strong> on Earth still has a mass of <strong>70 kg</strong> on the Moon. Mass never changes.</div>
    </div>

    <div style="background: #fff0f6; border-radius: 10px; padding: 16px; border: 1px solid #ffb3d1;">
      <div style="font-weight: 700; color: #1a1a2e; margin-bottom: 8px;">🌍 Weight changes</div>
      <div style="font-size: 14px; color: #495057; line-height: 1.6;">
        On Earth: <strong>70 × 9.8 = 686 N</strong><br/>
        On Moon: <strong>70 × 1.6 = 112 N</strong><br/>
        <span style="color: #f72585;">That's about ⅙ of their Earth weight!</span>
      </div>
    </div>

  </div>

<!-- IMPORTANT NOTE -->
<div style="background: #fffbea; border-radius: 12px; padding: 20px; margin-bottom: 24px; border-left: 4px solid #ffd60a; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.05);">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; font-weight: 700; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 1px; color: #e9c46a; margin-bottom: 8px;">⚠️ Common Misconception</div>
  <div style="font-size: 14px; color: #495057; line-height: 1.7;">
    In everyday language, people say <em>"I weigh 70 kg"</em> — but scientifically, that is your <strong>mass</strong>. Your <strong>weight</strong> would be expressed in Newtons. The confusion exists because on Earth's surface, gravity is roughly constant, so the two are often used interchangeably in daily life.
  </div>
</div>
