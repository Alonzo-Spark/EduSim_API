│ Definition                 │
│                             │
│ Work is the transfer of    │
│ energy that occurs when a  │
│ force acts on an object and │
│ the object moves in the    │
│ direction of the force.    │
│ SI unit: Joule (J).        │
└───────────────────────────┘
