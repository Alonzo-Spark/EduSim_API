## TOPIC: Laws of Thermodynamics

---

### [SECTION: Introduction]

Thermodynamics is the branch of physics that studies the relationships between **heat**, **work**, and **energy**. Its four fundamental laws govern every physical and chemical process in the universe — from engines to black holes.

---

### [SECTION: The Four Laws]

---

#### [LAW CARD: Zeroth Law]

**The Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics**

> *"If two systems are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other."*

**What it means:** This law defines **temperature** as a measurable, comparable property. It is the logical foundation for thermometers.

**Example:** If object A and object B are both at the same temperature as object C, then A and B are at the same temperature — even without direct contact.

---

#### [LAW CARD: First Law]

**The First Law of Thermodynamics**

> *"Energy cannot be created or destroyed — only converted from one form to another."*

**Also known as:** The Law of Conservation of Energy

### [FORMULA CARD: First Law]

$$\Delta U = Q - W$$

| Symbol | Meaning | SI Unit |
|--------|---------|---------|
| $\Delta U$ | Change in internal energy of the system | Joules (J) |
| $Q$ | Heat added **to** the system | Joules (J) |
| $W$ | Work done **by** the system | Joules (J) |

**What it means:** Any heat energy supplied to a system either increases its internal energy or is used to do work.

---

#### [LAW CARD: Second Law]

**The Second Law of Thermodynamics**

> *"The total entropy of an isolated system always increases over time, or remains constant in ideal reversible processes."*

**Also known as:** The Law of Entropy

### [FORMULA CARD: Second Law — Entropy]

$$\Delta S \geq \frac{Q}{T}$$

| Symbol | Meaning | SI Unit |
|--------|---------|---------|
| $\Delta S$ | Change in entropy | J/K |
| $Q$ | Heat transferred | Joules (J) |
| $T$ | Absolute temperature | Kelvin (K) |

**Key implications:**

- Heat naturally flows from **hot → cold**, never the reverse spontaneously
- No heat engine can be **100% efficient**
- The universe tends toward **disorder** (increasing entropy)

---

#### [LAW CARD: Third Law]

**The Third Law of Thermodynamics**

> *"As the temperature of a system approaches absolute zero, the entropy of the system approaches a minimum (constant) value."*

### [FORMULA CARD: Third Law]

$$\lim_{T \to 0} S = S_0$$

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| $T$ | Absolute temperature (Kelvin) |
| $S$ | Entropy of the system |
| $S_0$ | Minimum entropy (often taken as zero for a perfect crystal) |

**What it means:** It is **impossible** to reach absolute zero ($0\text{ K}$) in a finite number of steps. At $0\text{ K}$, atomic motion would cease entirely.
