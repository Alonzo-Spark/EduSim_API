┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Definition                │
│                           │
│ Pressure is the normal force exerted per unit area by a fluid on a surface. │
└───────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ SI Unit                   │
│                           │
│ The SI unit of pressure is the Pascal (Pa), equal to one newton per square metre (1 Pa = 1 N/m²). │
└───────────────────────────┘
