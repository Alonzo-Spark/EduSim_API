┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     Definition                        │
│                                                       │
│ Coulomb’s Law states that the electrostatic force      │
│ between two point charges is directly proportional to │
│ the product of the charges and inversely proportional │
│ to the square of the distance between them.           │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    Main Formula                       │
│                                                       │
│                $$F = k\,\frac{q_1\,q_2}{r^2}$$         │
