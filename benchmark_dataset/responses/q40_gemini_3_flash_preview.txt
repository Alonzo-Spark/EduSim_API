### 📘 MISSION BRIEFING: WAVE PROPAGATION

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Quick Definition                                                                                                   │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **Waves** are disturbances that transfer energy through a medium or vacuum without     │
#### │ the permanent displacement of matter. They are categorized by the direction of            │
#### │ particle oscillation relative to the direction of energy travel.                                          │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Key Concepts: The Two Modes                                                                              │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ 1. **Transverse Waves:** Oscillate perpendicular to the wave's path.                            │
#### │ 2. **Longitudinal Waves:** Oscillate parallel to the wave's path.                                  │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Main Formula: The Wave Equation                                                                        │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ $$ v = f \lambda $$                                                                                                        │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **[ Explain Formula ]**                                                                                                 │
#### │ $v$ = Velocity (m/s) | $f$ = Frequency (Hz) | $\lambda$ = Wavelength (m)                    │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **[ Open Formula Lab ]**                                                                                               │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Comparative Analysis                                                                                                 │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ | Feature | Transverse Waves | Longitudinal Waves |                                                  │
#### │ | :--- | :--- | :--- |                                                                                                     │
#### │ | **Particle Motion** | Perpendicular ($90^\circ$) | Parallel ($0^\circ$) |                              │
#### │ | **Structure** | Crests and Troughs | Compressions and Rarefactions |                            │
#### │ | **Medium** | Solids, Liquid surfaces, Vacuum | Solids, Liquids, and Gases |                      │
#### │ | **Pressure** | Constant pressure | Pressure and density changes |                                   │
#### │ | **Polarization** | Can be polarized | Cannot be polarized |                                             │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Characteristics                                                                                                         │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **Transverse Waves:**                                                                                                   │
#### │ • Require shear strength; cannot propagate through the bulk of a fluid (liquid/gas).       │
#### │ • Energy is moved by "up and down" or "side to side" motion.                                        │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **Longitudinal Waves:**                                                                                                 │
#### │ • Depend on the elasticity of the medium (compressibility).                                            │
#### │ • Energy is moved by a "push and pull" effect.                                                                  │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Applications & Examples                                                                                           │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **Transverse Examples:**                                                                                             │
#### │ • Light waves (Electromagnetic Spectrum)                                                                      │
#### │ • Ripples on a pond surface                                                                                           │
#### │ • Vibrating guitar strings                                                                                                │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ **Longitudinal Examples:**                                                                                            │
#### │ • Sound waves in air                                                                                                      │
#### │ • Ultrasound waves                                                                                                         │
#### │ • P-waves (Primary) in earthquakes                                                                             │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘

---

#### ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
#### │ Important Notes                                                                                                        │
#### │                                                                                                                                    │
#### │ • **Seismic Overlap:** Earthquakes produce both S-waves (Transverse) and                 │
#### │   P-waves (Longitudinal). P-waves arrive first because they travel faster.                      │
#### │ • **The Vacuum Rule:** Only electromagnetic transverse waves can travel through          │
#### │   a vacuum. Longitudinal waves always require a physical medium.                                 │
#### └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
