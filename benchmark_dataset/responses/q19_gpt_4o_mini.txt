### Derivation of Kinetic Energy Expression

1. **Work-Energy Principle**: The work done on an object is equal to the change in its kinetic energy.
   
2. **Work Done (W)**: If a constant force \( F \) acts on an object to move it a distance \( d \), the work done is given by:
   $$ W = F \cdot d $$

3. **Newton's Second Law**: The force can be expressed as \( F = ma \), where \( m \) is the mass and \( a \) is the acceleration.

4. **Acceleration Relation**: If an object starts from rest and accelerates uniformly, we can relate distance, initial velocity (\( u \)), final velocity (\( v \)), and acceleration (\( a \)) using the equation:
   $$ v^2 = u^2 + 2ad $$

   Since \( u = 0 \) (starting from rest), it simplifies to:
   $$ v^2 = 2ad $$

5. **Substituting for Work**: We can express \( d \) in terms of \( v \) and \( a \):
   $$ d = \frac{v^2}{2a} $$

6. **Substituting into Work**:
   $$ W = F \cdot d = ma \cdot \frac{v^2}{2a} = \frac{mv^2}{2} $$

Thus, the kinetic energy \( KE \) of the object is given by:
$$ KE = \frac{1}{2} mv^2 $$
