### 🔵 **First Law: Law of Inertia**
**Statement**:  
> "An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced external force."

**Key Concept**:  
Inertia (resistance to change in motion).

**Examples**:  
- 🚗 **Car braking**: Passengers lurch forward when a car stops suddenly (bodies resist change from motion to rest).  
- 🪑 **Chair push**: A stationary chair won't move until you apply force.  
- 🪐 **Planetary motion**: Planets orbit indefinitely in space (minimal external forces).

---

### 🧮 **Second Law: Force and Acceleration**
**Statement**:  
> "The acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass."

**Formula**:  
```math
\vec{F} = m\vec{a}
```

| Symbol | Quantity             | Unit           |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|
| \(F\)  | Net force            | Newton (N)     |
| \(m\)  | Mass                 | Kilogram (kg)  |
| \(a\)  | Acceleration         | m/s²           |

**Examples**:  
- ⚽ **Kicking a ball**: Light soccer ball accelerates more than a heavy bowling ball with the same force.  
- 🚚 **Truck acceleration**: A loaded truck accelerates slower than an empty one under identical engine force.  
- 🪂 **Skydiving**: Gravity (\(F\)) causes downward acceleration (\(a\)); mass (\(m\)) remains constant.

---

### ⚖️ **Third Law: Action-Reaction**
**Statement**:  
> "For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction."

**Key Concept**:  
Forces always occur in pairs (equal magnitude, opposite direction).

**Examples**:  
- 🚀 **Rocket launch**: Exhaust gases push down (action), rocket pushes up (reaction).  
- 🏊 **Swimming**: Pushing water backward (action) propels you forward (reaction).  
- 📚 **Book on table**: Book exerts downward force (action); table exerts upward normal force (reaction).

---

### 📊 **Laws Comparison**
| Law         | Core Principle                     | Real-World Application         |
|-------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| **First**   | Inertia governs motion             | Seatbelts in vehicles          |
| **Second**  | \(F = ma\) quantifies motion change | Rocket thrust calculations     |
| **Third**   | Force pairs are inseparable        | Walking/jumping mechanics      |

---

### 💎 **Summary**
1. **1st Law**: Objects resist motion changes (inertia).  
2. **2nd Law**: Force accelerates mass (\(F = ma\)).  
3. **3rd Law**: Forces occur in equal/opposite pairs.  

> 🔍 **Think deeper**: Why don’t action-reaction forces cancel each other? *(Hint: They act on different objects!)*
