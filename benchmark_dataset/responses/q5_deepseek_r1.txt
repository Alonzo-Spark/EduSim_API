To calculate the retardation of the train, we use the **kinematic equation for acceleration**:

**Formula:**
\[
a = \frac{v - u}{t}
\]

**Where:**
- \( v = \text{final velocity} = 0 \, \text{m/s} \) (train stops)
- \( u = \text{initial velocity} = 20 \, \text{m/s} \)
- \( t = \text{time} = 10 \, \text{s} \)

**Calculation:**
\[
a = \frac{0 - 20}{10} = -2 \, \text{m/s}^2
\]

**Interpretation:**
- The negative sign indicates **deceleration** (retardation).
- Magnitude of retardation = \( 2 \, \text{m/s}^2 \).

**Answer:** The retardation is \( \boxed{2 \, \text{m/s}^2} \).

---

**Key Concepts Summary:**
- Retardation is the rate of decrease in velocity.
- Standard acceleration formula applies, but final velocity is zero in braking scenarios.
- Retardation is reported as a positive value (magnitude of deceleration).
