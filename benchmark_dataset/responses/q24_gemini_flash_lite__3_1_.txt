**Introduction**

We will calculate the gravitational force between two objects. This force is always attractive, pulling objects towards each other.

---

**Formula**

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Main Formula              │
│                           │
│  $$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$      │
│                           │
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

**Variables and Values**

*   **F**: Gravitational Force (Newtons, N) - *This is what we are calculating.*
*   **G**: Gravitational Constant = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N⋅m²/kg² - *A fundamental constant.*
*   **m₁**: Mass of object 1 = 5 kg
*   **m₂**: Mass of object 2 = 5 kg
*   **r**: Distance between the centers of the masses = 2 m

---

**Calculation**

Let's plug the values into the formula:

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11} \frac{N⋅m^2}{kg^2}) \frac{(5 kg)(5 kg)}{(2 m)^2}$$

$$F = (6.674 \times 10^{-11} \frac{N⋅m^2}{kg^2}) \frac{25 kg^2}{4 m^2}$$

$$F = 4.171 \times 10^{-10} N$$

---

**Answer**

The gravitational force between the two masses is approximately:

$4.171 \times 10^{-10} N$
