### Given Data
- Current (I): 2 A  
- Resistance (R): 10 Ω  

### Calculation
To find the voltage (V), we use the formula:

\[
V = I \times R
\]

Substituting the given values:

\[
V = 2 \, \text{A} \times 10 \, \Omega = 20 \, \text{V}
\]
