    <h2 style="margin-top: 0; color: #1a73e8; font-size: 1.2rem; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 1px;">Introduction</h2>
    <p>When an object is raised against the force of gravity, work is done on it. This work is stored in the object as <strong>Gravitational Potential Energy (GPE)</strong>. The amount of energy depends on the object's mass, the height it reaches, and the strength of gravity.</p>
