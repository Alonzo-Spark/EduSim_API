Let's break down the calculation of the gravitational force using Newton's law of universal gravitation. 

### Introduction
The gravitational force between two objects can be calculated using the formula:

$$
F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}
$$

Where:
- \( F \) is the gravitational force,
- \( G \) is the gravitational constant (\( 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{N m}^2/\text{kg}^2 \)),
- \( m_1 \) and \( m_2 \) are the masses of the two objects,
- \( r \) is the distance between the centers of the two masses.

### Given Data
- Mass \( m_1 = 5 \, \text{kg} \)
- Mass \( m_2 = 5 \, \text{kg} \)
- Distance \( r = 2 \, \text{m} \)

### Calculation
Now substituting the values into the formula:

\[
F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \frac{(5)(5)}{(2)^2}
\]

Calculating it step-by-step:

1. Calculate \( m_1 m_2 = 5 \times 5 = 25 \).
2. Calculate \( r^2 = 2^2 = 4 \).
3. Now plug these values into the formula:

\[
F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \frac{25}{4}
\]

4. Divide \( 25 \) by \( 4 \):

\[
\frac{25}{4} = 6.25
\]

5. Now multiply by \( G \):

\[
F = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \times 6.25 = 4.17125 \times 10^{-10} \, \text{N}
\]

### Summary
The gravitational force between the two masses of 5 kg each placed 2 m apart is approximately:

\[
F \approx 4.17 \times 10^{-10} \, \text{N}
\]
