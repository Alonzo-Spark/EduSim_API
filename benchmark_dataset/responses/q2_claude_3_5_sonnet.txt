## Uniform Acceleration Problem

<div class="card intro-card">
  <div class="card-label">📘 What's Happening</div>
  <p>A car begins at <strong>rest</strong> (zero initial velocity) and gains speed at a <em>constant rate</em>. We need to find how fast it's moving after a set time.</p>
</div>

<div class="card definition-card">
  <div class="card-label">📖 Key Terms</div>
  <div class="term-grid">
    <div class="term-item">
      <span class="term-name">Uniform Acceleration</span>
      <span class="term-desc">Velocity increases by the same amount every second</span>
    </div>
    <div class="term-item">
      <span class="term-name">Starts from Rest</span>
      <span class="term-desc">Initial velocity u = 0 m/s</span>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card given-card">
  <div class="card-label">📋 Given Information</div>
  <div class="given-grid">
    <div class="given-row">
      <span class="given-symbol">u</span>
      <span class="given-equals">=</span>
      <span class="given-value">0 m/s</span>
      <span class="given-note">starts from rest</span>
    </div>
    <div class="given-row">
      <span class="given-symbol">a</span>
      <span class="given-equals">=</span>
      <span class="given-value">2 m/s²</span>
      <span class="given-note">uniform acceleration</span>
    </div>
    <div class="given-row">
      <span class="given-symbol">t</span>
      <span class="given-equals">=</span>
      <span class="given-value">10 s</span>
      <span class="given-note">time elapsed</span>
    </div>
    <div class="given-row find-row">
      <span class="given-symbol">v</span>
      <span class="given-equals">=</span>
      <span class="given-value">?</span>
      <span class="given-note">find this</span>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card formula-card">
  <div class="card-label">⚡ Formula Used</div>
  <div class="formula-name">First Equation of Motion</div>
  <div class="formula-display" id="formula-main">
    \[ v = u + at \]
  </div>
  <div class="formula-variables">
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var">v</span> = final velocity (m/s)</div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var">u</span> = initial velocity (m/s)</div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var">a</span> = acceleration (m/s²)</div>
    <div class="var-row"><span class="var">t</span> = time (s)</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card solution-card">
  <div class="card-label">🔢 Step-by-Step Solution</div>
  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">1</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <div class="step-label">Write the formula</div>
      <div class="step-math">\[ v = u + at \]</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">2</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <div class="step-label">Substitute the values</div>
      <div class="step-math">\[ v = 0 + (2)(10) \]</div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">3</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <div class="step-label">Simplify</div>
      <div class="step-math">\[ v = 20 \text{ m/s} \]</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card answer-card">
  <div class="answer-label">✅ Final Answer</div>
  <div class="answer-value">\[ v = 20 \text{ m/s} \]</div>
  <div class="answer-note">The car is travelling at <strong>20 metres per second</strong> after 10 seconds.</div>
</div>

<div class="card insight-card">
  <div class="card-label">💡 Quick Insight</div>
  <p>Since the car accelerates at <strong>2 m/s²</strong>, it gains <strong>2 m/s of speed every second</strong>. After 10 seconds, that's simply 2 × 10 = 20 m/s. The zero initial velocity made this especially clean!</p>
</div>

</div>
