│   Projectile Motion       │  
│       Introduction        │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

When a ball is launched into the air at an angle, it follows a curved path known as a **projectile motion**. The horizontal distance it travels before returning to the same vertical level is called the **range**.

In this case, the ball is projected at an angle of $ 45^\circ $ with an initial velocity of $ 20 \,\text{m/s} $. We can calculate the range using a standard formula from kinematics, assuming no air resistance and constant gravitational acceleration.

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│      Key Formula          │  
│                           │  
│   $ R = \dfrac{u^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g} $   │  
│                           │  
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │  
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

Where:  
- $ R $ = horizontal range (in meters)  
- $ u $ = initial velocity = $ 20 \,\text{m/s} $  
- $ \theta $ = angle of projection = $ 45^\circ $  
- $ g $ = acceleration due to gravity ≈ $ 9.8 \,\text{m/s}^2 $  

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│      Step-by-Step         │  
│     Calculation            │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

1. Plug in the values:  
$$
R = \frac{(20)^2 \sin(2 \times 45^\circ)}{9.8}
$$

2. Simplify:  
$$
R = \frac{400 \cdot \sin(90^\circ)}{9.8}
$$

3. Since $ \sin(90^\circ) = 1 $:  
$$
R = \frac{400 \cdot 1}{9.8} = \frac{400}{9.8}
$$

4. Final result:  
$$
R \approx 40.82\,\text{m}
$$

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│       Final Answer        │  
│                           │  
│   $ \boxed{40.82} \,\text{m} $   │  
│                           │  
│ The range of the ball is  │  
│ approximately 40.82 meters.│  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### ✅ Important Notes  
- The maximum range for a given speed occurs at $ 45^\circ $, which is why this angle was chosen.  
- This result assumes ideal conditions: no air resistance, flat ground, and constant $ g $.  
- The time of flight could also be calculated if needed, but wasn't required here.
