# Surface Tension

<div class="section-grid">

<div class="card definition-card">
  <div class="card-label">📖 Quick Definition</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    <strong>Surface Tension</strong> is the property of a liquid surface that makes it behave like a stretched elastic membrane, due to the <em>net inward cohesive force</em> acting on surface molecules.
    <br/><br/>
    It is defined as the <strong>force per unit length</strong> acting along the surface of a liquid, perpendicular to any imaginary line drawn on the surface.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card formula-card">
  <div class="card-label">⚗️ Main Formula</div>
  <div class="card-body formula-body">
    <div class="formula-display">
      $$T = \frac{F}{L}$$
    </div>
    <div class="formula-variables">
      <div class="var-row"><span class="var">T</span><span class="var-desc">Surface Tension (N/m)</span></div>
      <div class="var-row"><span class="var">F</span><span class="var-desc">Force acting on the surface (N)</span></div>
      <div class="var-row"><span class="var">L</span><span class="var-desc">Length of the line on the surface (m)</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card concept-card">
  <div class="card-label">💡 Physical Interpretation</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    <ul>
      <li>Molecules <strong>inside</strong> a liquid are pulled equally in all directions → zero net force.</li>
      <li>Molecules <strong>at the surface</strong> have fewer neighbors above → net inward pull.</li>
      <li>This inward pull causes the surface to <em>contract and minimize its area</em>.</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="highlight-box">
      SI Unit: <strong>N/m</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; CGS Unit: <strong>dyne/cm</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; Dimensions: <strong>[MT⁻²]</strong>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

</div>

---

<div class="section-header">⚙️ Factors Affecting Surface Tension</div>

<div class="section-grid">

<div class="card factor-card">
  <div class="card-label">🌡️ Temperature</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    As temperature <strong>increases</strong>, cohesive forces between molecules weaken.
    <div class="result-box decrease">
      ↓ Surface Tension decreases with rise in temperature
    </div>
    <em>Exception: Molten cadmium and copper show an increase.</em>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card factor-card">
  <div class="card-label">🧪 Impurities / Solutes</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Soluble impurities</strong> (e.g., NaCl, sugar) → <span class="up">↑ Increase</span> surface tension</li>
      <li><strong>Sparingly soluble impurities</strong> (e.g., soap, detergent) → <span class="down">↓ Decrease</span> surface tension</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="highlight-box">
      Surfactants like soap drastically reduce surface tension — used in cleaning.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card factor-card">
  <div class="card-label">🔗 Nature of Liquid</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    Liquids with <strong>stronger intermolecular (cohesive) forces</strong> have higher surface tension.
    <div class="comparison-row">
      <span class="badge high">Mercury — High ST</span>
      <span class="badge low">Alcohol — Low ST</span>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card factor-card">
  <div class="card-label">⚡ Electrification</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    When a liquid is <strong>electrified</strong>, charges on the surface repel each other, causing the surface to expand.
    <div class="result-box decrease">
      ↓ Surface Tension decreases on electrification
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card factor-card">
  <div class="card-label">🌊 Presence of Dissolved Gas</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    Dissolved gases in a liquid <strong>reduce cohesion</strong> between molecules.
    <div class="result-box decrease">
      ↓ Surface Tension decreases with dissolved gases
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card factor-card">
  <div class="card-label">🫧 Contamination / Surface Films</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    Oil films or dust on the surface reduce intermolecular attraction at the surface layer.
    <div class="result-box decrease">
      ↓ Surface Tension decreases with surface contamination
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

</div>

---

<div class="card summary-card">
  <div class="card-label">📋 Quick Summary Table</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    <div class="table-wrapper">

| Factor | Effect on Surface Tension |
|---|---|
| Increase in Temperature | ↓ Decreases |
| Soluble impurities (NaCl) | ↑ Increases |
| Surfactants (Soap) | ↓ Decreases |
| Electrification | ↓ Decreases |
| Dissolved Gases | ↓ Decreases |
| Stronger cohesive forces | ↑ Increases |

  </div>
  </div>
</div>
