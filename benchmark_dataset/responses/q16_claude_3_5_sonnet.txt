# Work, Energy and Power

---

## What is Work?

In physics, **work** is said to be done when a **force applied on an object causes displacement** in the direction of the force.

---

## Definition

> Work is defined as the product of the force applied on an object and the displacement produced in the direction of the force.

---

## Formula

$$W = F \cdot d \cdot \cos\theta$$

**Where:**

| Symbol | Meaning | SI Unit |
|--------|---------|---------|
| $W$ | Work done | Joule (J) |
| $F$ | Applied Force | Newton (N) |
| $d$ | Displacement | Metre (m) |
| $\theta$ | Angle between Force and Displacement | Degrees / Radians |

---

## SI Unit of Work

$$1 \text{ Joule} = 1 \text{ Newton} \times 1 \text{ Metre}$$

$$1 \text{ J} = 1 \text{ N·m}$$

> **The SI unit of Work is the Joule (J)**, named after the physicist **James Prescott Joule**.

**1 Joule** is defined as the work done when a force of **1 Newton** displaces an object by **1 metre** in the direction of the force.

---

## Special Cases

| Condition | Angle $\theta$ | Work Done |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Force and displacement in **same direction** | $0°$ | $W = Fd$ (Maximum) |
| Force applied at an **angle** | $0° < \theta < 90°$ | $W = Fd\cos\theta$ |
| Force **perpendicular** to displacement | $90°$ | $W = 0$ |
| Force **opposite** to displacement | $180°$ | $W = -Fd$ (Negative Work) |

---

## Key Conditions for Work to be Done

Work is done **only if** all three conditions are met:

- ✅ A **force** must be applied
- ✅ The object must undergo **displacement**
- ✅ There must be a **component of force** in the direction of displacement

---

## Quick Example

A person pushes a box with a force of **10 N** and it moves **3 metres** in the same direction.

$$W = F \times d = 10 \times 3 = 30 \text{ J}$$

> **Work done = 30 Joules**
