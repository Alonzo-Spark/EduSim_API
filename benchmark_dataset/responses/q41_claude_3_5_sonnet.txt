---

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #1a1a2e 0%, #16213e 50%, #0f3460 100%); border-radius: 16px; padding: 32px; color: white; margin-bottom: 24px;">
  <div style="font-size: 13px; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 3px; color: #e94560; margin-bottom: 8px;">Electrostatics</div>
  <div style="font-size: 32px; font-weight: 800; margin-bottom: 12px;">Coulomb's Law</div>
  <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #a8b2d8; line-height: 1.6;">The fundamental law governing the force between electric charges — formulated by Charles-Augustin de Coulomb in 1785.</div>
</div>

---

<div style="background: #0d1117; border: 1px solid #30363d; border-left: 4px solid #58a6ff; border-radius: 12px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #58a6ff; margin-bottom: 12px;">📖 Statement</div>
  <div style="font-size: 16px; color: #e6edf3; line-height: 1.8;">
    The electrostatic force of attraction or repulsion between two <strong style="color:#ffa657;">point charges</strong> is:
    <ul style="margin-top: 12px; color: #c9d1d9; line-height: 2;">
      <li><strong style="color:#ffa657;">Directly proportional</strong> to the product of the magnitudes of the two charges</li>
      <li><strong style="color:#ffa657;">Inversely proportional</strong> to the square of the distance between them</li>
      <li>Acting along the <strong style="color:#ffa657;">line joining</strong> the two charges</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div style="background: #0d1117; border: 1px solid #30363d; border-radius: 12px; padding: 28px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #3fb950; margin-bottom: 16px;">⚡ Main Formula</div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 24px; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; border: 1px solid #3fb950;">
    <div style="font-size: 22px; color: #e6edf3; margin-bottom: 8px;">

$$F = k \frac{q_1 \, q_2}{r^2}$$

</div>
    <div style="font-size: 13px; color: #8b949e; margin-top: 8px;">Scalar (magnitude) form</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 24px; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; border: 1px solid #e94560;">
    <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #8b949e; margin-bottom: 8px;">Vector Form</div>
    <div style="font-size: 20px; color: #e6edf3;">

$$\vec{F}_{12} = k \frac{q_1 \, q_2}{r^2} \, \hat{r}_{12}$$

</div>
    <div style="font-size: 13px; color: #8b949e; margin-top: 8px;">where $\hat{r}_{12}$ is the unit vector from charge 1 to charge 2</div>
  </div>

</div>

<div style="background: #0d1117; border: 1px solid #30363d; border-radius: 12px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #ffa657; margin-bottom: 16px;">🔗 Coulomb's Constant in Detail</div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 20px; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 16px;">

$$k = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} = 9 \times 10^9 \text{ N·m}^2\text{·C}^{-2}$$

</div>

  <div style="display: flex; gap: 16px; flex-wrap: wrap;">
    <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 200px; background: #161b22; border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; border: 1px solid #30363d;">
      <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 12px; margin-bottom: 6px;">$\varepsilon_0$ — Permittivity of Free Space</div>
      <div style="color: #e6edf3; font-size: 14px;">$8.85 \times 10^{-12}$ C²·N⁻¹·m⁻²</div>
    </div>
    <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 200px; background: #161b22; border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; border: 1px solid #30363d;">
      <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 12px; margin-bottom: 6px;">In a medium with permittivity $\varepsilon$</div>
      <div style="color: #e6edf3; font-size: 14px;">$F = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon} \cdot \dfrac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

---

<div style="background: #0d1117; border: 1px solid #30363d; border-radius: 12px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #58a6ff; margin-bottom: 16px;">⚖️ Key Characteristics</div>

  <div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr)); gap: 16px;">

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px; border-top: 3px solid #3fb950;">
    <div style="font-size: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">↔️</div>
    <div style="color: #3fb950; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 6px;">Newton's Third Law</div>
    <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 13px;">Forces on both charges are equal in magnitude, opposite in direction.</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px; border-top: 3px solid #58a6ff;">
    <div style="font-size: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">📏</div>
    <div style="color: #58a6ff; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 6px;">Inverse Square Law</div>
    <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 13px;">Force decreases with the square of distance — double $r$, force drops to ¼.</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px; border-top: 3px solid #d2a8ff;">
    <div style="font-size: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">🔵</div>
    <div style="color: #d2a8ff; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 6px;">Central Force</div>
    <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 13px;">Acts along the line joining the two point charges.</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px; border-top: 3px solid #ffa657;">
    <div style="font-size: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">🌌</div>
    <div style="color: #ffa657; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 6px;">Conservative Force</div>
    <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 13px;">Work done is path-independent; potential energy is well-defined.</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px; border-top: 3px solid #e94560;">
    <div style="font-size: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">🧱</div>
    <div style="color: #e94560; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 6px;">Superposition Principle</div>
    <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 13px;">Net force on a charge = vector sum of all individual Coulomb forces.</div>
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 10px; padding: 18px; border-top: 3px solid #79c0ff;">
    <div style="font-size: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">📌</div>
    <div style="color: #79c0ff; font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 6px;">Point Charges Only</div>
    <div style="color: #8b949e; font-size: 13px;">Strictly valid for point charges or spherically symmetric charge distributions.</div>
  </div>

  </div>
</div>

---

<div style="background: #0d1117; border: 1px solid #30363d; border-radius: 12px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-size: 11px; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #e94560; margin-bottom: 16px;">⚠️ Important Notes</div>

  <div style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: 12px;">

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 8px; padding: 14px 18px; border-left: 3px solid #ffa657; color: #c9d1d9; font-size: 14px;">
    🔶 Coulomb's Law holds in <strong style="color:#ffa657;">vacuum/free space</strong>. In a medium, $\varepsilon_0$ is replaced by $\varepsilon = \varepsilon_r \varepsilon_0$, where $\varepsilon_r$ is the relative permittivity (dielectric constant).
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 8px; padding: 14px 18px; border-left: 3px solid #58a6ff; color: #c9d1d9; font-size: 14px;">
    🔷 The sign of $F$ tells you the nature: <strong style="color:#3fb950;">positive F → repulsion</strong>, <strong style="color:#e94560;">negative F → attraction</strong>.
  </div>

  <div style="background: #161b22; border-radius: 8px; padding: 14px 18px; border-left: 3px solid #d2a8ff; color: #c9d1d9; font-size: 14px;">
    🟣 Coulomb's Law is analogous to <strong style="color:#d2a8ff;">Newton's Law of Gravitation</strong>, but gravity is always attractive while electrostatic force can be both.
  </div>

  </div>
</div>

  </div>
