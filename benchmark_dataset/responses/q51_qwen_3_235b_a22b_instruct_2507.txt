# Interactive Pendulum Lab: Mass vs. Period

## Quick Definition

A **simple pendulum** consists of a point mass suspended from a fixed support by a massless, inextensible string. When displaced from equilibrium and released, it oscillates under gravity.

---

## Key Concept

### Does Mass Affect Pendulum Period?

**Answer:** No — the mass of the bob does **not** affect the period of a simple pendulum.

Let’s explore why.
