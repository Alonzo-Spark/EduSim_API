# Wave Properties

## Three Fundamental Properties

---

### 📏 Wavelength

<div class="definition-card">

**Wavelength** is the distance between two consecutive points that are in phase on a wave — such as crest to crest or trough to trough.

</div>

<div class="formula-card">

**Symbol & Unit**

$$\lambda \quad \text{(lambda)} \quad [\text{meters, m}]$$

</div>

---

### 🔁 Frequency

<div class="definition-card">

**Frequency** is the number of complete wave cycles that pass a given point per unit time.

</div>

<div class="formula-card">

**Symbol & Unit**

$$f \quad [\text{Hz} = \text{cycles per second} = \text{s}^{-1}]$$

</div>

---

### 📶 Amplitude

<div class="definition-card">

**Amplitude** is the maximum displacement of a point on the wave from its equilibrium (rest) position.

</div>

<div class="formula-card">

**Symbol & Unit**

$$A \quad [\text{meters, m}]$$

</div>

---

## How They Connect

<div class="formula-card">

**The Wave Equation**

$$v = f \lambda$$

where $v$ is wave speed, $f$ is frequency, and $\lambda$ is wavelength.

</div>

---

## Comparison Table

| Property | Symbol | Unit | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wavelength | $\lambda$ | m | Spatial length of one cycle |
| Frequency | $f$ | Hz | Cycles per second |
| Amplitude | $A$ | m | Maximum displacement |
| Wave Speed | $v$ | m/s | Distance travelled per second |

---

## Quick Visual

```
        A ↑  *       *
           * | *   * | *
  ─────────*─┼─*─*─┼─*─────
           * | *   * | *
        A ↓  *       *
           |←──λ──→|
```

- **A** = Amplitude (height from centre)
- **λ** = Wavelength (length of one full cycle)
- **f** = how many full cycles pass per second

---

> 💡 **Key Insight:** Frequency and wavelength are **inversely proportional** at constant wave speed — a higher frequency means a shorter wavelength.
