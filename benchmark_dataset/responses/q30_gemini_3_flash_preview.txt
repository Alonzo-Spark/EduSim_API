### 📘 Physics Module: Fluid Dynamics
# Bernoulli’s Principle & Applications

---

### ┌ Introduction ──────────────────────────┐
│ Bernoulli’s Theorem is the fundamental principle of fluid dynamics describing the conservation of energy in a flowing fluid. It states that for an incompressible, non-viscous fluid in a steady flow, the sum of pressure energy, kinetic energy, and potential energy per unit volume remains constant along a streamline.                                               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

### ┌ Key Concepts ──────────────────────────┐
│ • **Ideal Fluid:** Assumes zero viscosity and incompressibility.          │
│ • **Steady Flow:** Velocity at any fixed point remains constant over time.│
│ • **Energy Conservation:** Mechanical energy is converted between forms.  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

### ┌ Main Formula ──────────────────────────┐
│                                                                           │
│  $$P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho gh = \text{Constant}$$                  │
│                                                                           │
│  **Variables:**                                                           │
│  • $P$: Static Pressure of the fluid                                      │
│  • $\rho$: Density of the fluid                                           │
│  • $v$: Flow velocity                                                     │
│  • $g$: Acceleration due to gravity                                       │
│  • $h$: Elevation (height)                                                │
│                                                                           │
│  [ 🔬 Open Formula Lab ]                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

### ┌ Characteristics ────────────────────────┐
│ 1. **Inverse Relationship:** As the speed of a moving fluid increases, the pressure within the fluid decreases (and vice versa).          │
│ 2. **Streamline Constraint:** The theorem applies strictly to points along the same streamline in laminar flow.                           │
│ 3. **Work-Energy Link:** The work done on the fluid by pressure equals the change in its kinetic and potential energy.                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

### ┌ Applications ──────────────────────────┐
│                                                                           │
│ **1. Aerofoil (Airplane Wing)**                                           │
│ The curved top of a wing forces air to travel faster over the top than    │
│ underneath. Higher velocity = Lower pressure. This pressure difference    │
│ creates upward **Lift**.                                                  │
│                                                                           │
│ **2. Venturi Meter**                                                      │
│ A device used to measure the flow speed of liquids in a pipe by           │
│ constricting the flow and measuring the resulting pressure drop.           │
│                                                                           │
│ **3. Bunsen Burner / Atomizers**                                          │
│ High-speed gas or air creates a low-pressure zone that "sucks" in fuel    │
│ or liquid (Venturi effect).                                               │
│                                                                           │
│ **4. Torricelli’s Law**                                                   │
│ Calculates the speed of efflux (fluid leaking from a tank).               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

### ┌ Comparison: Pressure vs. Velocity ──────┐
│                                                                           │
| Condition | Velocity ($v$) | Pressure ($P$) | Energy Form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wide Pipe** | Low | High | Potential/Static |
| **Narrow Pipe** | High | Low | Kinetic |
│                                                                           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

### ┌ Important Notes ───────────────────────┐
│ ⚠️ **Limitations:**                                                       │
│ • It ignores internal friction (viscosity).                               │
│ • It assumes the fluid density is constant (not applicable to high-speed  │
│   gas flows where air compresses).                                        │
│ • It does not account for thermal energy exchange.                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
