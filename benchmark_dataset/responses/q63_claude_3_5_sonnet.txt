### Core Governing Equations

**Frictional Force**

$$
f = \mu \cdot N
$$

**Deceleration from Friction**

$$
a = -\mu g
$$

**Stopping Distance**

$$
d = \frac{v_0^2}{2\mu g}
$$

**Time to Stop**

$$
t = \frac{v_0}{\mu g}
$$
