┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Quick Definition**      │  
│                           │  
│ Mechanical energy—the sum of kinetic and potential energy in a system—remains constant in the absence of non-conservative forces like friction or air resistance. This is the **principle of conservation of mechanical energy**. │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### 🔍 **Key Concepts**  

- **Mechanical Energy (E)** = Kinetic Energy (KE) + Potential Energy (PE)  
- **Conservation Principle**: If only conservative forces (like gravity) act, total mechanical energy stays constant.  
- Applies in idealized, frictionless environments.  

---

### 📐 **Main Formula**  

$$
KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f
$$

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Formula Card**          │  
│                           │  
│ $ KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f $ │  
│                           │  
│ - $ KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 $: Kinetic Energy  
│ - $ PE = mgh $: Gravitational Potential Energy  
│ - $ i $: initial state, $ f $: final state

### 🌟 **Example: Roller Coaster Drop**  

Imagine a roller coaster car at the top of a hill:  

- **Mass**: 500 kg  
- **Height**: 40 m  
- **Initial speed**: 0 m/s (starts from rest)  
- **Bottom height**: 0 m  
- **Assume no friction or air resistance**  

#### Step 1: Top of the Hill  
$$
PE_i = mgh = 500 \times 9.8 \times 40 = 196,000\ \text{J}  
$$  
$$
KE_i = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 500 \times 0^2 = 0\ \text{J}  
$$  
$$
E_i = 196,000\ \text{J}
$$

#### Step 2: Bottom of the Hill  
$$
PE_f = mgh = 500 \times 9.8 \times 0 = 0\ \text{J}  
$$  
All potential energy converts to kinetic energy:  
$$
KE_f = 196,000\ \text{J} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2  
$$  
Solve for $ v $:  
$$
196,000 = \frac{1}{2} \times 500 \times v^2  
\Rightarrow v^2 = \frac{196,000 \times 2}{500} = 784  
\Rightarrow v = \sqrt{784} = 28\ \text{m/s}
$$

✅ **Energy is conserved**:  
Initial mechanical energy = Final mechanical energy  
196,000 J = 196,000 J

---

### 🧩 **Energy Transformation**  

┌─────────────────────────────┐  
│ **Energy Conversion Chain** │  
├─────────────────────────────┤  
│ Potential Energy → Kinetic Energy  
│ (At top)             → (At bottom)  
└─────────────────────────────┘  

No energy is lost—only transformed.

---

### ⚠️ **Important Notes**  

- **Real-world systems** lose some mechanical energy to heat and sound (non-conservative forces).  
- Conservation **only holds perfectly** when friction, air resistance, and other dissipative forces are negligible.  
- This principle is foundational in physics, engineering, and design of rides, pendulums, and orbital mechanics.

---

### ✅ **Summary**  

- Mechanical energy is conserved when only conservative forces act.  
- Energy transforms between kinetic and potential forms.  
- Total amount remains constant: $ KE + PE = \text{constant} $.
