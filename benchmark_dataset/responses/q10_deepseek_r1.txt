### Scalar vs. Vector Quantities  
Physics distinguishes quantities by whether they include direction. Here's a clear breakdown:  

#### 🟥 **Scalar Quantities**  
- **Definition**: Fully described by **magnitude only** (a number + unit).  
- **Examples**:  
  - Mass: `5 kg` (no direction).  
  - Temperature: `25°C` (only how hot/cold).  
  - Time: `10 seconds` (duration).  
  - Distance: `100 m` (path length, ignoring direction).  
- **Math**: Scalars follow ordinary algebra.  
  > Example: `3 kg + 4 kg = 7 kg`  

#### 🟦 **Vector Quantities**  
- **Definition**: Described by **magnitude + direction**.  
- **Examples**:  
  - Displacement: `100 m North` (direction matters).  
  - Force: `20 N downward` (e.g., gravity).  
  - Velocity: `60 km/h East` (speed + direction).  
  - Acceleration: `9.8 m/s² downward`.  
- **Math**: Vectors require vector algebra.  
  > Example: Moving `3 m East` + `4 m North` → Resultant displacement = `5 m Northeast`.  

---

### Key Differences  
| **Aspect**          | **Scalar**                          | **Vector**                          |  
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|  
| **Direction**        | ❌ Not required                     | ✅ Required                         |  
| **Representation**   | Number + unit (e.g., `50 J`)        | Arrow (e.g., `→` for force)         |  
| **Addition**         | Arithmetic sum (`5 + 3 = 8`)        | Geometric (tip-to-tail method)      |  
| **Dependence**       | Independent of coordinates          | Changes with coordinate system      |  
| **Examples**         | Mass, time, energy, density         | Velocity, force, momentum, torque   |  

---

### ⚠️ Common Pitfalls  
1. **Speed vs. Velocity**:  
   - Speed (scalar): `60 km/h`.  
   - Velocity (vector): `60 km/h North`.  
2. **Distance vs. Displacement**:  
   - Distance (scalar): Total path length (e.g., `500 m`).  
   - Displacement (vector): Straight-line from start to end (e.g., `50 m East`).  

---

### Summary  
| **Type**     | **Defining Trait** | **Physics Impact**          |  
|--------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|  
| **Scalar**   | Magnitude only     | Describes "how much"        |  
| **Vector**   | Magnitude + direction | Describes "how much + where" |  

> 🔍 **Test Yourself**:  
> - Is *electric charge* scalar or vector? (Ans: Scalar — no direction).  
> - Is *weight* scalar or vector? (Ans: Vector — it acts downward).
