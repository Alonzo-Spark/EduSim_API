### Understanding Latent Heat and Change of State

#### 🔍 Quick Definition  
**Latent heat** is the "hidden" energy absorbed or released by a substance during a **phase change** (e.g., solid to liquid) *without changing its temperature*.  

---

#### 🧩 Key Concepts  
1. **Change of State**  
   - Occurs when matter transitions between **solid, liquid, or gas** phases.  
   - Driven by **energy transfer** (heating/cooling).  
   - **Temperature remains constant** during the phase change.  

2. **Latent Heat Types**  
   | Type                | Symbol | Definition                          | Example                     |  
   |---------------------|--------|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------|  
   | **Fusion**          | \(L_f\)| Heat to melt/freeze 1 kg of solid   | Ice → Water (0°C)           |  
   | **Vaporization**    | \(L_v\)| Heat to vaporize/condense 1 kg liquid| Water → Steam (100°C)       |  
   | **Sublimation**     | \(L_s\)| Heat for solid → gas directly       | Dry ice → CO₂ gas           |  

---

#### ⚖️ The Physics: Energy Transfer  
- **Why no temperature change?**  
  Energy breaks/makes **intermolecular bonds** instead of increasing molecular kinetic energy.  
  - *Melting*: Breaks rigid bonds → liquid freedom.  
  - *Vaporization*: Overcomes all bonds → gas expansion.  

---

#### 📐 Formula for Latent Heat  
The energy (\(Q\)) absorbed/released during a phase change is:  

<div class="formula-card">  
<h3>Latent Heat Equation</h3>  
$$ Q = m \cdot L $$  
<p>Where:<br>  
\(Q\) = Energy transferred (Joules),<br>  
\(m\) = Mass of substance (kg),<br>  
\(L\) = Specific latent heat (J/kg).  
</p>  
</div>  

> ⚡ **Example**: Melting 2 kg of ice (\(L_f = 334,000  \text{J/kg}\)):  
> $$ Q = 2 \times 334,000 = 668,000  \text{J} $$  

---

#### ❄️ Real-World Applications  
1. **Sweating cools you**: Water absorbs \(L_v\) from skin to evaporate.  
2. **Ice packs**: Ice absorbs \(L_f\) from injuries during melting.  
3. **Pressure cookers**: Raise boiling point → reduces \(L_v\) needed.  

---

#### ⚠️ Important Notes  
- **Cooling curves vs. heating curves**: Temperature plateaus during phase changes.  
- \(L_v > L_f\): Vaporization requires ~7× more energy than fusion (for water).  
- **Conservation**: Energy released in freezing = energy absorbed in melting.  

---

#### 💎 Summary  
- **Latent heat** = Energy for phase changes at constant temperature.  
- **Change of state** involves bond restructuring, not temperature change.  
- **Formula**: \( Q = m \cdot L \) quantifies the "hidden" energy.
