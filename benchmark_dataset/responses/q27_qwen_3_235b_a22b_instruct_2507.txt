┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Quick Definition**      │  
│                           │  
│ **Pascal’s Law** states that a pressure change applied to an enclosed, incompressible fluid is transmitted undiminished to every portion of the fluid and to the walls of the container.  
└───────────────────────────┘  

---

### 🔍 **Key Concept**  
Pascal’s Law is a fundamental principle in **fluid mechanics**, particularly in **hydraulics**. It emphasizes that pressure applied at any point in a confined fluid spreads equally in all directions.

---

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ **Main Formula**          │  
│                           │  
│ $$  
P = \frac{F}{A}  
$$  │  
│                           │  
│ ▪ $P$ = Pressure  
│ ▪ $F$ = Force applied  
│ ▪ $A$ = Area over which force is applied  
│  
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │  
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

> **Note:** Since pressure is transmitted equally, a small force on a small-area piston can generate a large force on a larger-area piston—this is the basis of hydraulic systems.

---

### ⚙️ **Applications of Pascal’s Law**

┌───────────────────────┐  
│ **Hydraulic Lift**    │  
│ A small force on a small piston creates enough pressure to lift heavy objects (e.g., cars in repair shops) using a larger piston.  
└───────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────┐  
│ **Hydraulic Brakes**  │  
│ Used in vehicles—pressing the brake pedal applies pressure through fluid, uniformly engaging brakes on all wheels.  
└───────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────┐  
│ **Hydraulic Press**   │  
│ Used in manufacturing to compress materials like metal or plastic using high fluid-generated force.  
└───────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────┐  
│ **Hydraulic Jacks**   │  
│ Enable lifting heavy loads (e.g., machinery) with minimal input force.  
└───────────────────────┘  

---

### ✅ **Important Notes**  
- Applies only to **incompressible fluids** (like liquids, not gases).  
- The fluid must be **confined** (in a closed system).  
- The principle relies on **pressure transmission**, not force multiplication—force increases due to larger area ($F = P \times A$).

---

### 📚 **Summary**  
Pascal’s Law explains how pressure is uniformly transmitted in confined fluids. It forms the foundation of **hydraulic systems**, enabling force amplification with minimal input—revolutionizing transportation, industry, and engineering.
