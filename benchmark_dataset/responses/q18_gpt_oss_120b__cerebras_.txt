┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Introduction                                                     │
│                                                                   │
│ In this example we determine the gravitational potential       │
│ energy of a body that is lifted vertically.                       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Definition                                                       │
│                                                                   │
│ **Gravitational Potential Energy (PE)** is the energy stored   │
│ in an object due to its position in a gravitational field.      │
│ It depends on the object's mass, the height above a reference   │
│ level, and the local acceleration due to gravity (g).           │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Key Concepts                                                     │
│                                                                   │
│ • **Mass (m)** – amount of matter (kg)                           │
│ • **Height (h)** – vertical displacement from the reference     │
│   point (m)                                                       │
│ • **Gravity (g)** – acceleration due to Earth’s gravity         │
│   (≈ 9.81 m s⁻²)                                                   │
│ • **Potential Energy (PE)** – energy stored because of position │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Main Formula                                                     │
│                                                                   │
│          PE = m · g · h                                         │
│                                                                   │
│ [ Explain Formula ]   [ Open Formula Lab ]                       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Calculation                                                      │
│                                                                   │
│ Given:                                                            │
│   • m = 5 kg                                                      │
│   • h = 10 m                                                     │
│   • g ≈ 9.81 m s⁻²                                                │
│                                                                   │
│ PE = 5 kg × 9.81 m s⁻² × 10 m                                      │
│ PE = 490.5 J                                                     │
│                                                                   │
│ **Result:** The body possesses **≈ 4.9 × 10² J** of potential     │
│ energy after being lifted 10 m.                                   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Summary                                                          │
│                                                                   │
│ • Gravitational potential energy is calculated with PE = mgh.   │
│ • For a 5 kg mass raised 10 m, PE ≈ 4.9 × 10² J.                 │
│ • The calculation hinges on the local value of g (≈ 9.81 m s⁻²). │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
