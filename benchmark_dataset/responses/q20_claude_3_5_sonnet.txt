Here's an interactive breakdown of Conservation of Mechanical Energy:

---

<div class="content-blocks">

<div class="info-card definition-card">
<div class="card-label">📖 Core Concept</div>

**Conservation of Mechanical Energy** states that in a system where only **conservative forces** act (like gravity or spring force), the total mechanical energy remains constant throughout the motion.

</div>

---

<div class="info-card formula-card">
<div class="card-label">⚡ The Law</div>

$$E_{mechanical} = KE + PE = \text{constant}$$

$$\frac{1}{2}mv_1^2 + mgh_1 = \frac{1}{2}mv_2^2 + mgh_2$$

<div class="formula-variables">

| Symbol | Meaning | Unit |
|--------|---------|------|
| $m$ | Mass of object | kg |
| $v_1, v_2$ | Speed at point 1 and 2 | m/s |
| $g$ | Acceleration due to gravity | m/s² |
| $h_1, h_2$ | Height at point 1 and 2 | m |

</div>
</div>

---

<div class="info-card example-card">
<div class="card-label">🎯 Classic Example — Free Falling Ball</div>

Imagine a ball of mass **2 kg** dropped from a height of **10 m**.

**At the Top (Point A):**
- Height $h_1 = 10\ m$
- Velocity $v_1 = 0\ m/s$
- $KE_A = \frac{1}{2}(2)(0)^2 = 0\ J$
- $PE_A = mgh_1 = (2)(10)(10) = 200\ J$
- $\boxed{E_{total} = 200\ J}$

**At the Bottom (Point B):**
- Height $h_2 = 0\ m$
- $PE_B = 0\ J$
- By conservation: $KE_B = 200\ J$
- $\frac{1}{2}(2)v^2 = 200 \Rightarrow v = \sqrt{200} \approx 14.1\ m/s$
- $\boxed{E_{total} = 200\ J}$

</div>

---

<div class="info-card visual-card">
<div class="card-label">📊 Energy Transformation at Each Stage</div>

| Position | Height | KE | PE | Total Energy |
|----------|--------|----|----|-------------|
| Top | 10 m | 0 J | 200 J | 200 J |
| Midpoint | 5 m | 100 J | 100 J | 200 J |
| Bottom | 0 m | 200 J | 0 J | 200 J |

> 🔄 Notice how KE and PE trade off perfectly — their **sum never changes**.

</div>

---

<div class="info-card condition-card">
<div class="card-label">✅ Conditions Required</div>

- No **friction** or **air resistance**
- Only **conservative forces** do work
- System is **isolated** (no external energy added or removed)

> ⚠️ If friction acts, mechanical energy is **not** conserved — some converts to heat. We then use the **Work-Energy Theorem** instead.

</div>

---

<div class="info-card applications-card">
<div class="card-label">🌍 Real-World Applications</div>

| Scenario | Energy Conversion |
|----------|------------------|
| Roller coaster | PE → KE → PE (repeating) |
| Pendulum swing | PE ↔ KE continuously |
| Hydroelectric dam | PE of water → KE → Electrical |
| Bow and arrow | Elastic PE → KE of arrow |

</div>

---

<div class="info-card summary-card">
<div class="card-label">💡 Key Takeaway</div>

Energy is **never created or destroyed** — it only **transforms** between kinetic and potential forms. The total mechanical energy of an isolated system stays the same at every point in its motion.

$$\Delta KE + \Delta PE = 0$$

</div>
