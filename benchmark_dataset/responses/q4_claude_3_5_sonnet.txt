<!-- CORE PRINCIPLE -->
<div style="background: #fffbea; border: 1px solid #f0c040; border-radius: 12px; padding: 20px 24px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <div style="font-size: 12px; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: 1.5px; color: #b8860b; text-transform: uppercase; margin-bottom: 10px;">💡 Core Principle</div>
  <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #444; line-height: 1.7;">
    The <strong>displacement</strong> of an object equals the <strong>area under its velocity–time graph</strong>.<br>
    For uniformly accelerating motion, the v–t graph is a straight line — so the area is a <em>trapezoid</em> (rectangle + triangle).
  </div>

<!-- STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION -->
<div style="background: white; border: 1px solid #e0e0e0; border-radius: 12px; padding: 24px; margin-bottom: 20px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06);">
  <div style="font-size: 12px; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: 1.5px; color: #555; text-transform: uppercase; margin-bottom: 20px;">🔢 Step-by-Step Derivation</div>

  <!-- Step 1 -->
  <div style="display:flex; gap:16px; margin-bottom:24px; align-items:flex-start;">
    <div style="background:#2d6a9f; color:white; border-radius:50%; width:32px; height:32px; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; font-weight:700; font-size:14px; flex-shrink:0;">1</div>
    <div style="flex:1;">
      <div style="font-weight:600; color:#1e3a5f; margin-bottom:6px; font-size:15px;">Area = Rectangle + Triangle</div>
      <div style="color:#555; font-size:14px; line-height:1.7; margin-bottom:10px;">
        The total area under the v–t graph is the sum of the rectangle (base) and the triangle (top):
      </div>
      <div style="background:#f8f9fa; border-radius:8px; padding:14px; text-align:center;">
        $$s = \underbrace{u \cdot t}_{\text{Rectangle}} + \underbrace{\frac{1}{2}(v - u) \cdot t}_{\text{Triangle}}$$
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 2 -->
  <div style="display:flex; gap:16px; margin-bottom:24px; align-items:flex-start;">
    <div style="background:#2d6a9f; color:white; border-radius:50%; width:32px; height:32px; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; font-weight:700; font-size:14px; flex-shrink:0;">2</div>
    <div style="flex:1;">
      <div style="font-weight:600; color:#1e3a5f; margin-bottom:6px; font-size:15px;">Use the First Equation of Motion</div>
      <div style="color:#555; font-size:14px; line-height:1.7; margin-bottom:10px;">
        From <strong>v = u + at</strong>, we know that:
      </div>
      <div style="background:#f8f9fa; border-radius:8px; padding:14px; text-align:center;">
        $$v - u = at$$
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 3 -->
  <div style="display:flex; gap:16px; margin-bottom:24px; align-items:flex-start;">
    <div style="background:#2d6a9f; color:white; border-radius:50%; width:32px; height:32px; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; font-weight:700; font-size:14px; flex-shrink:0;">3</div>
    <div style="flex:1;">
      <div style="font-weight:600; color:#1e3a5f; margin-bottom:6px; font-size:15px;">Substitute into the Area Expression</div>
      <div style="color:#555; font-size:14px; line-height:1.7; margin-bottom:10px;">
        Replace \((v - u)\) with \(at\):
      </div>
      <div style="background:#f8f9fa; border-radius:8px; padding:14px; text-align:center;">
        $$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}(at) \cdot t$$
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Step 4 -->
  <div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:flex-start;">
    <div style="background:#27ae60; color:white; border-radius:50%; width:32px; height:32px; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; font-weight:700; font-size:14px; flex-shrink:0;">4</div>
    <div style="flex:1;">
      <div style="font-weight:600; color:#1e3a5f; margin-bottom:6px; font-size:15px;">Simplify</div>
      <div style="color:#555; font-size:14px; line-height:1.7; margin-bottom:10px;">
        Multiply \(t \cdot t = t^2\):
      </div>
      <div style="background:#f0fdf4; border: 2px solid #27ae60; border-radius:8px; padding:14px; text-align:center;">
        $$\boxed{s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2}$$
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
