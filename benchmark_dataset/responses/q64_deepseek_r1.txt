**Runtime Overlay Response: Spring Stretched (Peak Limit Event)**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**🔍 Quick Definition**  
A spring reaches its *peak limit* when stretched beyond the elastic deformation range, entering plastic deformation where Hooke's Law no longer applies.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**📜 Main Formula**  
**Hooke's Law (Pre-Limit):**  
$$ F = -kx $$  

**Peak Limit Condition:**  
$$ x > x_{\text{elastic}} $$  

**Key Variables**  
| Symbol | Quantity | SI Unit | Critical Value |  
|--------|----------|---------|----------------|  
| *F* | Restoring force | N | Varies |  
| *k* | Spring constant | N/m | Material-dependent |  
| *x* | Displacement | m | **Critical:** x_elastic |  

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**⚠️ Key Consequences**  
1. **Permanent deformation** - Spring won't return to original length  
2. **Hysteresis** - Altered force-displacement relationship  
3. **Energy dissipation** - Work done isn't fully recoverable  

**Real-World Impact**  
- Suspension system failure  
- Mattress spring breakdown  
- Mechanical watch inaccuracy  

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
