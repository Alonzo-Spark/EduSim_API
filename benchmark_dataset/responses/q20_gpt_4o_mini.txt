┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Introduction               │  
│                           │  
│ The conservation of mechanical energy states that in a closed system where only conservative forces are acting, the total mechanical energy remains constant. This means that the sum of potential energy and kinetic energy remains unchanged over time.  │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Key Concepts              │  
│                           │  
│ - **Mechanical Energy**: The sum of kinetic and potential energy in a system.  │  
│ - **Kinetic Energy (KE)**: Energy of an object due to its motion, given by the formula:  
│   $$ KE = \frac{1}{2} mv^2 $$  
│ - **Potential Energy (PE)**: Energy stored in an object due to its position, often gravitational, given by:  
│   $$ PE = mgh $$  
└───────────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Example                  │  
│                           │  
│ Consider a simple pendulum. At its highest point, the pendulum has maximum potential energy and zero kinetic energy. As it swings down, potential energy is converted into kinetic energy. At the lowest point, the kinetic energy is at its maximum while potential energy is at its minimum. When it swings up again, kinetic energy converts back into potential energy.  │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Main Formula              │  
│                           │  
│ The conservation of mechanical energy can be expressed as:  
│ $$ KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f $$

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Applications              │  
│                           │  
│ - Engineering (roller coasters)  
│ - Physics (projectile motion)  
│ - Natural phenomena (waterfalls)  
└───────────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Important Notes          │  
│                           │  
│ - Non-conservative forces (like friction) can cause mechanical energy to be transformed into thermal energy, affecting the total mechanical energy of the system.  
└───────────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Summary                  │  
│                           │  
│ The conservation of mechanical energy is a fundamental principle in physics that illustrates how energy can change forms but is never created or destroyed in a closed system.  │  
└───────────────────────────┘
