## Newton's Second Law of Motion

<div class="cards-grid">

<div class="card card-definition">
  <div class="card-label">📘 Quick Definition</div>
  <div class="card-content">
    <strong>Newton's Second Law</strong> states that the acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="card card-formula">
  <div class="card-label">⚡ Core Formula</div>
  <div class="card-content formula-display">

$$F = ma$$

  </div>
  <div class="formula-vars">
    <span class="var-chip"><strong>F</strong> = Net Force (N)</span>
    <span class="var-chip"><strong>m</strong> = Mass (kg)</span>
    <span class="var-chip"><strong>a</strong> = Acceleration (m/s²)</span>
  </div>
</div>

</div>

---

<div class="card card-solution">
  <div class="card-label">🔢 Step-by-Step Solution</div>
  <div class="card-content">

**Given:**

| Quantity | Symbol | Value |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Force | F | 50 N |
| Mass | m | 10 kg |
| Acceleration | a | ? |

**Rearranging the formula:**

$$a = \frac{F}{m}$$

**Substituting values:**

$$a = \frac{50}{10}$$

$$\boxed{a = 5 \text{ m/s}^2}$$

  </div>
</div>

<div class="card card-answer">
  <div class="card-label">✅ Final Answer</div>
  <div class="card-content">
    The acceleration of the body is <strong>5 m/s²</strong> in the direction of the applied force.
  </div>
</div>

</div>
