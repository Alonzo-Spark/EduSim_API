### Momentum and Conservation of Momentum

#### ⚡ Quick Definition  
**Momentum** is a measure of an object's motion, combining its mass and velocity. **Conservation of Momentum** states that in a closed system with no external forces, total momentum remains constant.

---

#### 📖 Core Concept  
- **Momentum (\( \vec{p} \))** is a vector quantity:  
  \[
  \vec{p} = m \vec{v}
  \]  
  - \( m \): Mass (kg)  
  - \( \vec{v} \): Velocity (m/s)  
- **Direction matters**: Momentum points in the direction of velocity.

---

#### 🔍 Conservation of Momentum  
**Law**: For a system with **no net external force**, total momentum before and after an interaction (e.g., collision) is conserved:  
\[
\sum \vec{p}_{\text{initial}} = \sum \vec{p}_{\text{final}}
\]

**Derivation** (Using Newton’s Laws):  
1. **Newton’s Third Law**: Forces between two objects are equal and opposite:  
   \[
   \vec{F}_{1 \to 2} = -\vec{F}_{2 \to 1}
   \]  
2. **Newton’s Second Law**: Force changes momentum (\( \vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} \)).  
3. For two colliding objects:  
   \[
   \frac{d\vec{p}_1}{dt} = -\frac{d\vec{p}_2}{dt} \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{d}{dt}(\vec{p}_1 + \vec{p}_2) = 0
   \]  
4. Integrate over time:  
   \[
   \vec{p}_1 + \vec{p}_2 = \text{constant}
   \]  
   ∴ **Total momentum is conserved**.

---

#### 🌟 Key Applications  
1. **Collisions**:  
   - Elastic (kinetic energy conserved).  
   - Inelastic (kinetic energy not conserved, but momentum is).  
2. **Rocket Propulsion**:  
   Fuel ejection creates equal-and-opposite momentum change.  
3. **Physics Problems**:  
   Solve for unknown velocities post-collision.

---

#### 📊 Momentum in Different Collisions  
| **Collision Type** | **Momentum Conserved?** | **Kinetic Energy Conserved?** |  
|-------------------|------------------------|------------------------------|  
| Elastic           | ✅ Yes                 | ✅ Yes                       |  
| Inelastic         | ✅ Yes                 | ❌ No                        |  
| Perfectly Inelastic | ✅ Yes                 | ❌ No (objects stick together) |  

---

#### ⚠️ Important Notes  
- **Closed System Required**: External forces (e.g., friction) invalidate conservation.  
- **Vector Addition**: Momentum components (\( p_x, p_y, p_z \)) are independently conserved.  
- **Real-World Example**: Airbags increase collision time, reducing force (same momentum change).
