┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Quick Definition          │
│                           │
│ **Retardation** is negative acceleration — the rate at which velocity decreases over time. It is measured in m/s². │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

### Given Data

- Initial velocity, $ u = 20 \, \text{m/s} $
- Final velocity, $ v = 0 \, \text{m/s} $ (since the train stops)
- Time taken, $ t = 10 \, \text{s} $

---

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Main Formula              │
│                           │
│ $$                       │
│ v = u + at                │
$$                       │
│                           │
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

We use the first equation of motion:

$$
v = u + at
$$

Substitute the known values:

$$
0 = 20 + a \times 10
$$

Solve for $ a $:

$$
10a = -20
$$

$$
a = -2 \, \text{m/s}^2
$$

---

┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Result                    │
│                           │
│ The **retardation** is    │
│ $ 2 \, \text{m/s}^2 $.    │
│ (Note: We drop the minus  │
│ sign when stating          │
│ retardation, as it        │
│ implies deceleration.)    │
└───────────────────────────┘

---

### Key Concept

- **Acceleration** = change in velocity per unit time.
- **Retardation** = negative acceleration.
- Magnitude of retardation = $ |a| $

---

### Final Answer:
$$
\boxed{2 \, \text{m/s}^2}
$$
