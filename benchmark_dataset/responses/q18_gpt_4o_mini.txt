│ energy possessed by an    │  
│ object due to its position │  
│ relative to other objects. │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

┌───────────────────────────┐  
│ Main Formula              │  
│                           │  
│     $$PE = mgh$$         │  
│                           │  
│ [ Explain Formula ]       │  
│ [ Open Formula Lab ]      │  
└───────────────────────────┘  

### Given Data:
- Mass (m) = 5 kg  
- Height (h) = 10 m  
- Acceleration due to gravity (g) ≈ 9.81 m/s² (standard value)  

### Calculation:
Using the formula for potential energy:

\[
PE = mgh
\]

Substituting the values:

\[
PE = 5 \, \text{kg} \times 9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2 \times 10 \, \text{m}
\]

Calculating this gives:

\[
PE = 5 \times 9.81 \times 10 = 490.5 \, \text{J}
\]
